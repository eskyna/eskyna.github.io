## Definition

**Vamp Romantic** is a dark, sensual, and elegant style direction that combines romantic details with dramatic mood.

## In simple words

Typical elements include lace, velvet, satin, sheer fabrics, and deep shades such as black, bordeaux, plum, and aubergine. The key is to keep it intentional, not costume-like.

## ESKYNA extra: The Drama Dial

Use Vamp Romantic in levels:

1. subtle accent: dark nails, plum scarf, bordeaux bag
2. medium: lace blouse with straight jeans
3. stronger: velvet blazer or satin skirt
4. evening: corset details, long coat, deeper makeup

**ESKYNA line:** Vamp Romantic is not a costume. It is the art of dosing darkness with elegance.

## Styling tip

Start with one dramatic item and keep the rest clean, for example a lace top with tailored trousers and simple slingbacks.
