## Definition

**Japanese Sneakers** describes sneakers with a reduced, sporty, often retro-inspired look associated with Japanese design aesthetics.

## In simple words

**Japanese Sneakers** are usually quieter than chunky statement sneakers. Typical features are slimmer silhouettes, clear lines, and high-quality material mixes such as suede, leather, nylon, or mesh.

The term does not always mean production in Japan. It often refers to an aesthetic: functional, precise, understated, and detail-oriented.

## How to style it

They pair well with wide trousers, denim, tailored pants, maxi skirts, and simple dresses. Their reduced shape keeps the look sporty without becoming dominant.

## Styling tip

Style them with clean outfits: wide trousers, a simple shirt, and a short jacket create a modern balance.
