Bra fit describes how band, cups, bridge, underwire, and straps work together as one support system.

## Definition

Fit quality is not only size. It depends on compatibility between body shape and bra architecture: band tension, cup volume, wire width, bridge height, and strap position. Correct fit feels secure without painful pressure.

## In Simple Words

Good bra fit means stable support without constant adjustment.

## Why This Term Matters

Poor fit affects more than comfort. It changes garment fall, line clarity, and visual proportion.

## Consultation Perspective

In consultation, we evaluate fit in movement: raising arms, walking, sitting, and bending. That reveals whether the full system works.

## Practical Styling Use

- check that the band stays level and stable
- verify bridge and wire contact without pressure peaks
- test cup edge for cutting or gaping
- use straps for fine tuning, not main support
- verify line quality under different top fabrics

- test fit and movement
- check behavior in real light
- verify comfort over several hours
- combine with existing wardrobe pieces

## Concrete Example

A bra can look correct in static posture but fail after ten minutes of real movement. Fit testing catches that early.

## Typical Misunderstanding

Correct size label alone does not guarantee fit. Cup shape mismatch is often the true issue.

## Distinction from Similar Terms

Bra size measuring: gives a starting point, not final fit confirmation.

Plunge/Balconette/Push-up: different shape logics with different fit requirements.

Bralette: comfort-focused with different support structure.

## ESKYNA Key Takeaway

Fit is system compatibility, not just a number and letter.

## Decision Aid Before Purchase or Styling

- does the band stay stable all day
- do cups sit cleanly without cutting or gaping
- does posture support improve naturally
- do outfit lines look calmer with this fit
- can you wear it comfortably for hours

- does it fit your role and context
- does material behavior match your expectation
- can you combine it at least three ways

## When Consultation Helps

Consultation is especially useful for recurring fit problems, changing size phases, or complex outfit demands.
