## Definition

A **Short Jacket** is cut noticeably shorter than a classic jacket and usually ends at the waist or upper hip.

## In simple words

The **Short Jacket** is powerful because of proportion. It highlights the waist area and can visually lengthen the legs, especially with high-waist trousers, skirts, or dresses.

It appears in many forms: cropped denim jacket, cropped blazer, leather jacket, bomber jacket, bouclé jacket, or lightweight summer jacket.

## How to style it

A short jacket quickly structures an outfit. Over dresses it creates a clear line. With wide trousers it prevents the silhouette from feeling too heavy.

## Styling tip

Pair it with high waists, maxi skirts, wide jeans, or dresses. For a lengthening effect, choose a hemline near your natural waist.
