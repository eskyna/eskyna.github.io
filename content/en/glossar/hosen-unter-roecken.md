## Definition

**Pants under Skirts** describes a layered look where a skirt or dress is worn over trousers.

## In simple words

This styling idea plays with layers, lengths, and proportion. A skirt or dress is not worn alone but combined with pants underneath. What once looked strongly Y2K can feel mature today through cleaner cuts and calmer color choices.

The look works through contrast: flowing skirts over straight trousers, transparent dresses over jeans, or mini skirts over slim pants.

## How to style it

Keep proportions intentional. Too much volume can look heavy quickly. Balanced combinations use slimmer pants, longer skirts, and tone-on-tone palettes.

## Styling tip

Start with straight pants and a light flowing or semi-transparent skirt in a similar color. Add a simple top or short jacket to keep focus on layering.
