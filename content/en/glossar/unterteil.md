## Definition

A **bottom** is a separate garment worn on the lower half of the body. Common bottoms include [trousers](/en/glossar/hose/), jeans, [skirts](/en/glossar/rock/), [shorts](/en/glossar/shorts/), and [leggings](/en/glossar/leggings/).

In everyday fashion language, “top and bottom” describes the basic structure of an outfit. The top covers the upper body, while the bottom establishes the lower silhouette, waist placement, and much of the outfit’s comfort and practical purpose.

## Why Bottoms Matter in an Outfit

A bottom does more than cover the lower body. Its cut determines where the eye travels, how long the legs appear, how much movement the outfit allows, and whether the overall look feels relaxed, polished, sporty, or formal.

The most important details are usually the waistband, rise, fit through the hips and thighs, leg shape, fabric, and hem length. For example, [high-waist](/en/glossar/high-waist/) trousers can create a higher visual waistline, while a long [midi skirt](/en/glossar/midirock/) creates a different balance with the same top.

Bottoms also influence how easily an outfit comes together. A simple shirt can look casual with jeans, work-ready with tailored trousers, or more feminine with a skirt. This makes well-chosen bottoms essential [combination pieces](/en/glossar/kombinationsstueck/) in a functional wardrobe.

## Common Types of Bottoms

### Trousers and jeans

Trousers include tailored styles, chinos, wide-leg designs, cargo trousers, and many other cuts. Jeans are trousers made from denim and are often used as reliable everyday basics.

The shape of the leg changes the visual effect. A [straight leg](/en/glossar/straight-leg/) creates a relatively even line, while [wide-leg](/en/glossar/wide-leg/) trousers add volume and movement. A tapered or slim cut brings the line closer to the leg.

### Skirts

Skirts are separate lower-body garments that vary widely in length, volume, and shape. A pencil skirt, A-line skirt, pleated skirt, or wrap skirt can make the same blouse look entirely different.

Pay attention to [skirt length](/en/glossar/rocklange/) and fabric movement. A structured skirt often reads as more polished, while a soft, flowing skirt can create a relaxed or romantic effect.

### Shorts

Shorts are bottoms with separate legs that end above or around the knee. They range from casual denim shorts to tailored Bermuda shorts and dressier styles in linen, crepe, or satin.

The hem position, width, and material matter more than the label alone. A tailored pair can work for warm-weather city outfits, while a softer jersey short is usually more casual.

### Leggings and other stretch bottoms

Leggings are close-fitting stretch bottoms designed for comfort and movement. They can be practical for sport, travel, layering, or relaxed everyday outfits, depending on fabric opacity, construction, and styling.

Because they follow the body closely, check the fabric’s coverage and recovery. A well-fitting pair should move comfortably without becoming transparent or slipping down during wear.

## How to Choose the Right Bottom

Choose bottoms based on your real routine rather than a single trend. Consider where you will wear them, what footwear you use most, and which tops already work in your wardrobe.

Start with these questions:

- Does the waistband sit comfortably when standing and sitting?
- Does the fit allow you to walk, bend, and move naturally?
- Does the length work with the shoes you actually wear?
- Can you create at least three outfits with tops, layers, and accessories you already own?
- Does the silhouette support the balance you want in the outfit?

A useful bottom should feel good as well as look intentional. Fit is especially important around the waist, hips, seat, crotch, and upper thigh. Pulling lines, gaping waistbands, or fabric that twists while walking can indicate a poor [fit](/en/glossar/passform/), even if the size label seems correct.

## Using Bottoms to Shape Proportion

Bottoms are a practical tool for working with [proportion](/en/glossar/proportion/). The visual relationship between the top and bottom can make an outfit feel balanced, elongated, relaxed, or deliberately dramatic.

A shorter jacket or tucked-in shirt can highlight the waist and create a clearer division between the upper and lower body. A longer top over slim trousers produces a different, more vertical line. There is no universally correct formula. The goal is to choose proportions that feel comfortable and suit the intended occasion.

Color also matters. A bottom in a similar color to your shoes can create continuity through the lower half of the outfit. A contrasting color, bold print, or textured fabric can turn the bottom into a focal point.

## Common Misunderstandings

- **A bottom is only a pair of trousers.**  
  Bottom is an umbrella term for separate lower-body garments, including skirts, shorts, leggings, jeans, and trousers.

- **A smaller size always gives a cleaner look.**  
  A too-small bottom often creates pulling, restricted movement, and uncomfortable pressure. A good fit follows the body without strain.

- **One bottom should work for every situation.**  
  A versatile wardrobe benefits from a few different options. The ideal bottom for commuting, a formal meeting, exercise, and a summer dinner may not be the same garment.

- **The style name matters more than the fit.**  
  Labels such as wide-leg, straight-leg, or high-waist are useful guides, but fabric, construction, and individual proportions determine how a bottom actually wears.

## Practical Bottom Check

Before buying or wearing a bottom, assess it in motion rather than only in a fitting-room mirror.

- Sit down to check whether the waistband digs in, gaps, or slides.
- Walk several steps to see whether the fabric twists, rides up, or restricts movement.
- Check the hem with your usual shoes, not only barefoot.
- Look at the back and side view for pulling folds around the seat, hips, and thighs.
- Pair it with at least two tops from your wardrobe to test its [versatility](/en/glossar/kombinierbarkeit/).
- Consider whether the fabric and shape suit the occasion and expected dress code.

## ESKYNA Key Line

A good bottom is not simply a lower-body garment. It is the foundation that supports an outfit’s comfort, proportion, and versatility.
