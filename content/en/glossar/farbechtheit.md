## Definition

Depending on the load, a distinction is made between fastness to washing, rubbing, light, water or sweat. A material can be stable in one respect and delicate in another.

## In simple words

Dark denim can rub when rubbed, a bold shirt can bleed when washed, and a summer fabric can fade in the sun. Color fastness is therefore not a single all-or-nothing characteristic. Care, color depth, fiber and dyeing process all work together.

## What to pay attention to

- First wash strong colors separately and follow the care instructions.
- For critical parts, gently rub an inconspicuous area with a light, damp cloth.
- Protect sensitive colors from long periods of direct sunlight.

## Common misconceptions

An intense color does not automatically mean fake, and a high price does not guarantee complete color fastness.

## ESKYNA note

> Good color should not only impress in the store, but should also withstand washing, wearing and exposure to light as reliably as possible.
