## Definition

The term was particularly associated with business fashion of the 1980s: prominent shoulders, clear suits and controlled silhouettes. Today, power dressing can look much more diverse and personal.

## In simple words

Presence is not only created through hard cuts or dark colors. A calm trouser suit, a precise dress, high-quality knitwear or a consistent signature look can also convey strength. It is crucial that the outfit fits the context and does not demand more attention than the person would like to wear.

## What to pay attention to

- First define what effect you need: authority, calm, creativity or accessibility.
- Check whether the shoulder line, material and contrast support this statement.
- Make sure you can move and talk without adjusting your outfit.

## Common misconceptions

Power dressing does not mean dressing as strictly or masculine as possible. Power can also arise through calm, precision and recognizability.

## ESKYNA mnemonic

> Power dressing makes your role visible without masking your personality.
