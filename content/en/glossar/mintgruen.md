## Definition

**Mint Green** is a light, cool green tone with a fresh, airy, and summery effect.

## In simple words

**Mint Green** sits between green, pastel, and a touch of blue. It looks clean and light without feeling overly sweet. That makes it useful for summer outfits and modern accents.

With white it appears crisp. With beige and cream it feels softer. With silver, gray, or cool denim it can look more contemporary and slightly futuristic.

## How to style it

You do not need a full mint outfit. One piece, such as a bag, sandal, blouse, or scarf, is often enough to add freshness.

## Styling tip

Start with accessories if you want a simple entry. For a stronger styling statement, combine mint with cream and white in a tone-on-tone palette.
