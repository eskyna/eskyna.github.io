## Definition

**Balloon Trousers** are trousers with a rounded, voluminous shape through hip and thigh that tapers toward the hem.

## In simple words

They are not simply wide trousers. The silhouette is constructed and architectural, creating space around the body while keeping structure.

## ESKYNA extra: Wearable Space

Balloon Trousers are about giving the body room without hiding it.

**ESKYNA line:** Balloon Trousers are not just wide trousers. They are wearable space.

## Styling tip

Balance volume with cleaner tops such as fitted knits, tanks, or cropped blazers.
