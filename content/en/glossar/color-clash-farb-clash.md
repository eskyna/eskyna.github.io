## Definition

**Color Clash** is the intentional combination of colors that do not look classically harmonious at first glance.

## In simple words

The point is controlled tension, not random brightness. Unusual pairings can look strong and modern when shape and material stay clear.

## ESKYNA extra: Controlled Disobedience

Build your look with a main color, a disruptive color, and a peacemaker neutral.

**ESKYNA line:** Color Clash is not a color mistake. It is controlled disobedience.

## Styling tip

Start with one disruptive accessory color against a neutral base, then move to full two-color combinations.
