## Definition

**Underwear** is clothing worn directly on the skin and beneath visible garments. Depending on the item, it can provide coverage, comfort, hygiene, support, warmth, shaping, or protection from friction between the body and outer clothing.

The category includes bras, briefs, panties, underwear sets, undershirts, slips, camisoles, bodysuits, stockings, and shaping garments. Some pieces are purely practical, while others are designed to be decorative or intentionally visible as part of an outfit.

## Underwear Explained Simply

Good underwear is the foundation of how clothes feel and fit. A supportive bra can change the line of a blouse or dress, while briefs that stay in place can make a busy day much more comfortable. Underwear is usually unseen, but it can strongly influence confidence, movement, and the finish of an outfit.

The right choice depends on what you are wearing over it. A close-fitting knit dress may call for smooth edges and a seamless appearance. A white or sheer blouse may need underwear with enough coverage and a color that is less visible beneath the fabric. A strapless or open-back dress often requires a more specialized support solution.

Underwear does not need to be restrictive to work well. Comfort, breathable materials, secure construction, and an appropriate fit matter more than choosing the smallest size or the highest level of compression.

## Types of Underwear

Common types of underwear include:

- **Bras:** Provide varying levels of support, shaping, coverage, and lift. A well-fitting bra is especially important under fitted tops and dresses. Learn more about [Bra Fit](/en/glossar/bh-passform/).
- **Briefs and panties:** Cover the lower body in different cuts, rises, and coverage levels, from fuller briefs to more minimal styles.
- **Camisoles and undershirts:** Lightweight layers worn under shirts, blouses, knitwear, or jackets for coverage, warmth, or a smoother finish.
- **Bodysuits:** One-piece garments that cover the torso and fasten at the crotch. A [body](/en/glossar/body/) can create a neat tucked-in look.
- **Shapewear:** Supportive underwear designed to smooth or shape the body under clothing. It should feel firm but still allow comfortable breathing and movement. See [Shapewear](/en/glossar/shapewear/).
- **Lingerie:** Intimate apparel that may be functional, decorative, sensual, or all three. Explore the broader meaning of [Dessous and Lingerie](/en/glossar/dessous/).

## How Underwear Affects an Outfit

Underwear influences more than comfort. It affects the visible line, opacity, support, and overall polish of outer clothing.

### Fit and silhouette

A bra that fits well can help tops, dresses, and jackets sit as intended. Cups that are too small may create spillage, while a loose band often provides insufficient support. For everyday smoothness under T-shirts and fine knits, a [T-Shirt Bra](/en/glossar/t-shirt-bh/) can be a useful option.

The fit of briefs also matters. Underwear that is too tight can dig into the skin or create visible lines, while a style that is too loose may shift or bunch under trousers and skirts.

### Coverage and visibility

Lightweight, white, or semi-sheer garments can reveal underwear more easily. Check the outfit in natural light and move around before leaving home. The issue is not simply whether the underwear matches your skin tone, but whether the fabric, color, texture, and seams remain discreet under the specific garment.

For sheer clothing, visible underwear can also be intentional. In that case, choose the underlayer as part of the outfit rather than treating it as an accidental detail. This approach is closely related to [Sheer Dressing](/en/glossar/sheer-dressing/) and [Underwear as Outerwear](/en/glossar/underwear-as-outerwear/).

### Comfort and movement

Underwear should remain comfortable while sitting, walking, reaching, and bending. Waistbands should not roll, straps should not constantly slide, and seams should not rub. Consider the activity as well as the outfit: what works for a short dinner may not work for a full workday, travel, or an event with lots of movement.

## How to Choose Underwear

Start with the garment you plan to wear, then choose underwear around its practical needs.

1. **Check the outer fabric.** Fine jersey, silk, satin, and light colors tend to show seams, texture, and color more clearly than heavier fabrics.
2. **Choose support for the neckline.** A deep V-neck may need a [plunge bra](/en/glossar/plunge-bh/), while a strapless top may require a [strapless bra](/en/glossar/traegerloser-bh/).
3. **Prioritize fit over the label.** Sizing can vary across brands. Assess how the piece feels and moves rather than relying on the number or letter alone.
4. **Consider the rise and leg opening.** The waistband should sit comfortably beneath the garment's waistline, and the leg opening should not cut in or bunch.
5. **Match the level of shaping to your needs.** If you choose shapewear, select a level that feels wearable for the duration of the occasion.
6. **Build a practical rotation.** A small range of reliable styles for everyday wear, fitted outfits, special necklines, and occasions is usually more useful than many rarely worn pieces.

## Common Misunderstandings

- **Misunderstanding: Tight underwear gives better support.**  
  **Better:** Support comes from appropriate construction and fit. Excessively tight underwear can restrict movement, dig in, and become uncomfortable.

- **Misunderstanding: Nude underwear is always invisible.**  
  **Better:** Visibility depends on the relationship between the underwear, your skin, and the outer fabric. Test it under the actual garment and in daylight.

- **Misunderstanding: Bra straps should carry most of the support.**  
  **Better:** In most bras, the band provides the main support. Straps help stabilize the cups and should not have to do all the work.

- **Misunderstanding: Visible underwear lines always mean poor styling.**  
  **Better:** They can be unwanted under a smooth fitted outfit, but visible edges or lingerie details may also be a deliberate styling choice.

- **Misunderstanding: Shapewear is necessary for a polished outfit.**  
  **Better:** Shapewear is optional. Well-fitting clothing and comfortable underwear can look polished without additional compression.

## Practical Underwear Check

Before wearing a new underwear piece under an outfit, check the following:

- Can you sit, walk, raise your arms, and bend comfortably?
- Does the underwear stay in place without rolling, pinching, or shifting?
- Are bra cups smooth, supportive, and free from gaping or overflow?
- Does the color, texture, and seam placement work under the outer fabric?
- Is the waistband hidden where you want it to be?
- Does the level of support suit the activity and length of time you will wear it?

## ESKYNA Key Line

The best underwear is not necessarily the most noticeable or the most compressive. It is the layer that feels comfortable, supports your body, and lets your clothes sit as intended.

## Advisory View

Treat underwear as part of outfit planning, not an afterthought. A few well-fitting everyday options, a reliable smooth bra, and suitable solutions for special necklines can make dressing easier and reduce avoidable fit problems.
