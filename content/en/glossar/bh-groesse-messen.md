Measuring bra size means capturing underbust and bust dimensions correctly and using them as a fit starting point.

## Definition

You measure firmly under the bust and around the fullest bust point with neutral posture. The output is a starting recommendation. Final fit must be validated through cup shape, band behavior, and movement testing.

## In Simple Words

Measurement starts the process. Fit confirms the result.

## Why This Term Matters

Many people repeat outdated labels for years. Correct measuring shortens search time and reduces expensive mismatches.

## Consultation Perspective

In consultation, we combine measurements with fit checks across multiple bra constructions to build a realistic size strategy.

## Practical Styling Use

- use a flexible tape on bare skin or very thin bra
- take underbust firmly without pain
- measure bust in upright neutral posture
- test sister sizes instead of one label only
- always validate with movement and outfit context

- test fit and movement
- check behavior in real light
- verify comfort over several hours
- combine with existing wardrobe pieces

## Concrete Example

If the band feels right but the cup gaps at the top, size alone is not the full answer. Cup shape may be the key variable.

## Typical Misunderstanding

One measured size does not fit every brand and model equally.

## Distinction from Similar Terms

Bra fit: validates real performance in a specific model.

Sister sizes: similar cup volume with different band length.

Bralette sizing: often simplified and not directly equivalent.

## ESKYNA Key Takeaway

Measuring gives direction. Fit gives certainty.

## Decision Aid Before Purchase or Styling

- were both measurements taken cleanly and recently
- did you test more than one size option
- does the band stay stable in motion
- does cup shape match your bust base
- does the result work across your key bra types

- does it fit your role and context
- does material behavior match your expectation
- can you combine it at least three ways
