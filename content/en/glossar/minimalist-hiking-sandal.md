## Definition

A **Minimalist Hiking Sandal** is a reduced outdoor sandal with functional straps, a flat sole, and a sporty look.

## In simple words

The **Minimalist Hiking Sandal** is a cleaner version of classic trekking sandals. Instead of heavy technical styling, it focuses on narrow straps, clean lines, and a flatter silhouette. It stays practical while looking more urban.

Adjustable straps and comfortable soles make it suitable for daily wear. It works not only with casual outfits but also with dresses, linen trousers, shorts, and maxi skirts.

## How to style it

The appeal is in contrast. A sporty sandal instantly relaxes feminine, elegant, or very clean outfits and feels more current than many heeled summer shoes.

## Styling tip

Wear it with wide linen trousers, simple dresses, bermudas, or tone-on-tone looks. For a stronger contrast, pair it with a flowing skirt.
