## Definition

**Fisherman Sandals** are flat sandals with a closed or semi-closed strap structure and sturdy sole.

## In simple words

They offer more hold than very open sandals and add a grounded, structured feel to summer outfits.

## ESKYNA extra: The anti-sweet sandal

They balance romantic pieces and make soft looks feel more modern.

**ESKYNA line:** Fisherman Sandals are the small resistance against too much sweetness.

## Styling tip

Pair them with midi skirts, linen trousers, or simple dresses for a clean modern contrast.
