## Definition

3-Word Method refers to a contemporary styling idea with a clear visual cue. It is less about rigid rules and more about shaping a deliberate style direction.

## In Simple Words

3-Word Method adds a recognizable mood to an outfit through one focused idea.

## Styling Effect

When used with restraint, 3-Word Method adds clarity, personality, and a modern edge. The effect is strongest when one element leads and the rest remains understated.

## Practical Use

- Start with one key piece that represents 3-Word Method.
- Keep colors and silhouettes around it reduced.
- Repeat one detail, such as color, texture, or shape, to make the look coherent.

## Common Misconception

3-Word Method is often mistaken for a full costume concept. In practice, it works best as a styling accent within your existing wardrobe.

## Distinction

Unlike generic trend imitation, 3-Word Method becomes useful when it supports your proportions, context, and personal style vocabulary.

## ESKYNA Key Takeaway

A trend becomes wearable when it strengthens your line instead of replacing it.

## FAQ

### Is 3-Word Method suitable for everyday outfits?

Yes. Choose one core signal and pair it with reliable basics.

### How do you keep 3-Word Method modern instead of overdone?

Prioritize quality, proportion, and restraint. One strong choice is usually enough.
