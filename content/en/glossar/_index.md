## Style, Color & Wardrobe Glossary

The ESKYNA glossary is a practical reference for fashion and personal style. It explains the vocabulary of [style](/en/glossar/stil/), color analysis, clothing fit, materials, dress codes, and wardrobe planning in clear English.

This is not a dry fashion dictionary. Each term is designed to help with real decisions: choosing colors that support you, understanding why a garment feels wrong, recognizing quality details, and creating outfits that feel coherent and useful.

## What can you find in the glossary?

Use the glossary to explore:

- **Color and color analysis:** Learn about [undertone](/en/glossar/unterton/), [color temperature](/en/glossar/farbtemperatur/), [contrast](/en/glossar/kontrast/), and [color harmony](/en/glossar/farbharmonie/).
- **Fit, cut, and proportion:** Understand [fit](/en/glossar/passform/), [silhouette](/en/glossar/silhouette/), [proportion](/en/glossar/proportion/), lines, lengths, and volume.
- **Style and personal impact:** Find language for [personal style](/en/glossar/personal-style/), [presence](/en/glossar/praesenz/), [image](/en/glossar/image/), and the message your outfit communicates.
- **Wardrobe planning and shopping:** Build a more useful wardrobe through [wardrobe logic](/en/glossar/garderobenlogik/), [capsule wardrobes](/en/glossar/capsule-wardrobe/), [versatility](/en/glossar/kombinierbarkeit/), and thoughtful buying criteria.
- **Materials and garment details:** Explore fabrics, texture, care, construction, accessories, and the details that influence comfort, durability, and visual effect.
- **Dress codes and occasions:** Decode invitations and choose appropriate outfits for business settings, celebrations, evening events, and everyday life.

## How to use the ESKYNA fashion glossary

Start with the question behind your outfit decision.

If a color feels too strong, too pale, or somehow disconnected from you, begin with [color consultation](/en/glossar/farbberatung/) and [color type](/en/glossar/farbtyp/). If clothing fits technically but still does not look right, explore [cut](/en/glossar/schnitt/), [body shape](/en/glossar/koerperform/), and proportion.

When your wardrobe contains many individual pieces but few easy outfits, look at [wardrobe planning](/en/glossar/garderobenplanung/), [combination pieces](/en/glossar/kombinationsstueck/), and [outfit formulas](/en/glossar/outfit-formel/). For an invitation or professional setting, start with [dress code](/en/glossar/dresscode/) and occasion-specific terms.

**Choose your entry point: alphabetically, by category, by relevance, or through the style question you want to solve today.**

## From fashion vocabulary to confident decisions

Knowing a term is useful when it gives you a clearer next step. Understanding [waist emphasis](/en/glossar/taillenbetonung/) can help you assess a dress. Knowing the difference between [neutral colors](/en/glossar/neutrale-farben/) and an accent color can make outfit planning easier. Recognizing a fabric's [drape](/en/glossar/materialfall/) or [texture](/en/glossar/struktur/) can help you shop with more intention.

The goal is not to follow every fashion rule. It is to develop style clarity, make better use of what you own, and choose new pieces with greater confidence.
