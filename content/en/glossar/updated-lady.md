## Definition

**Updated Lady** is a modern interpretation of classic ladylike dressing with more ease, contrast, and present-day proportion.

## In simple words

It uses refined elements like structured bags, tailored jackets, midi skirts, and elegant shoes, then modernizes them through relaxed pairings and cleaner styling.

## ESKYNA extra: Soft Authority

Authority without hardness: polished, feminine, and grounded.

**ESKYNA line:** Updated Lady does not dress to look proper. She dresses to stay sovereign.

## Styling tip

Mix one classic ladylike item with one relaxed piece, for example a tweed blazer with denim.
