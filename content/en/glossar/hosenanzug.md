## Definition

Classically, the blazer and trousers are made from the same fabric. Modern trouser suits can be wide, fitted, soft, oversized, plain or patterned and range from business to evening.

## In simple words

The common material and color line stretches and appears cohesive. At the same time, both pieces can be worn individually, which increases their utility in the wardrobe. It is crucial that the jacket length, trouser shape and shoe height together form a harmonious proportion.

## What to pay attention to

- Check the shoulder fit, closure point and waistband separately - one set must fit on both parts.
- Test the suit with the shoes and tops you will actually wear.
- Pay attention to whether the fabric and color can also be combined as individual pieces.

## Common misconceptions

A trouser suit doesn't have to look strict or masculine. Material, color, silhouette and styling determine the statement.
