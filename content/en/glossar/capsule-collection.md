## Definition

It can be released seasonally, limited or as a special project and includes significantly fewer pieces than a full main collection. Often the focus is on a specific topic or collaboration.

## In simple words

A capsule collection is an offering from a brand; A capsule wardrobe, on the other hand, is a personal wardrobe concept made up of items that can be combined in many different ways. A branded capsule does not have to be minimalist or fully combinable with each other, even if it often appears compactly curated.

## What to pay attention to

- Check each item for utility in your existing wardrobe rather than for the appeal of limitation.
- Pay attention to whether the common idea also combines quality and function.
- Don't let artificial scarcity pressure you into making a bad purchase.

## Common misconceptions

Capsule collection and capsule wardrobe are not the same thing. One is a product collection, the other is a personal wardrobe system.

## ESKYNA note

> A small collection is only valuable if its clear theme fits your real needs.
