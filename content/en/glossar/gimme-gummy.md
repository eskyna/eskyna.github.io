## Definition

**Gimme Gummy** is a playful fashion aesthetic built on shiny, elastic-looking, and tactile surfaces.

## In simple words

Think jelly bags, translucent sandals, candy-color clips, and 3D jewelry. The look is less about classic elegance and more about texture, light, and touch.

## ESKYNA extra: Touchpoint Styling

Give each outfit one tactile focal point that feels almost touchable.

**ESKYNA line:** Gimme Gummy is the moment when an outfit is not only seen, but almost felt.

## Styling tip

Use one glossy accent with calm bases like white, denim, cream, or black so the trend stays wearable.
