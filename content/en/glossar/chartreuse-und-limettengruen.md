## Definition

**Chartreuse and Lime Green** is explained as a practical color decision, not only as a color label.

## In simple words

This term helps you evaluate undertone, brightness, saturation, and material impact before you buy or combine pieces.

## Advisory view

Test the shade near your face in daylight and compare it with adjacent tones. The right nuance makes your features clearer and your skin more balanced.

## Styling tip

Use Chartreuse and Lime Green either as a base tone or as a focused accent and pair it with one calm neutral for structure.
