## Definition

**Style Consultation**: Style consultation is a personal analysis of proportion, color, cut, material, occasion, and impact. It helps you understand which clothes not only fit, but also make your personality visible and your everyday decisions easier.

## In simple words

Style consultation answers questions such as: What truly suits me? Why does an outfit look dressed, but not like me? Which cuts work again and again? Which pieces are missing so that the clothes I already own become more useful?

## Why Style Consultation matters

Many wardrobes are full but not clear. Individual pieces may be beautiful, yet they do not create reliable outfits together. Style consultation creates a system: colors, silhouettes, materials, and occasions begin to connect. You shop more consciously, combine faster, and show up with more ease.

## ESKYNA perspective

At ESKYNA, I connect style consultation with personal presence. The work includes body lines, but also posture, profession, life phase, energy, and desired impression. A good style can be practical and still feel special. It does not need to be loud, but it should be recognizably yours.

## How to use this idea

- Collect outfits in which you immediately feel confident.
- Notice which colors, cuts, and materials appear repeatedly.
- Check where your real life differs from your ideal style image.
- Translate the patterns into buying criteria instead of vague inspiration.

## Everyday example

Many clients do not come because they do not care about clothes. They come because too many beautiful pieces fail to create one clear direction. A skirt feels feminine, the blazer serious, the sneakers practical, but together there is no image. Style consultation translates those pieces into a language: which of them truly show you and which belong to an older idea of yourself?

## When consultation helps

Consultation helps when similar shopping mistakes repeat or when your wardrobe no longer fits your life phase. An outside view recognizes patterns faster: too many in-between pieces, missing basics, wrong lengths, or a style image that no longer matches your everyday reality.

## Common misunderstanding

Style consultation is often confused with a makeover. A makeover may look dramatic, but it rarely solves everyday dressing. Good style consultation builds on what is already there and shows which changes create the strongest effect.

## Difference from related terms

Style consultation is wider than color consultation and more concrete than inspiration. Color consultation clarifies the color direction, image consulting clarifies external impact, and style consultation connects both with cuts, outfits, and wardrobe logic.

## Real consulting situation

Many people do not seek style consultation because they have no style. They seek it because too many directions pull at once: one look feels elegant, another creative, a third practical. Consultation sorts these impulses and shows which elements should repeat so outfits stop feeling random.

## Mini case: from doubt to decision

A typical example: the blazer should stay, but it should not feel strict. The solution may be a softer fabric, a better length, calmer colors, and a shoe that adds structure without making the whole look formal.

## Decision filter before your next purchase

Turn many style ideas into a personal direction that works in daily life. Use this short filter before you buy, remove pieces, or plan an outfit for an important situation.

- Collect five outfits in which you genuinely feel like yourself.
- Mark repeated lines, colors, materials, and levels of formality.
- Remove trends that only work in pictures but not in your real week.
- Define three style words that can filter future purchases.

## Signs this topic needs support

Support becomes useful when you collect a lot of inspiration but it does not turn into reliable decisions. One sign is copying outfits and still not feeling like yourself in them. In consultation, no style is imposed on you. The work reveals which lines, colors, and details can carry your personality repeatedly.
