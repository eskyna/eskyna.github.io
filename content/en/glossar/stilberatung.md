## Definition

**Style consultation** is personalized style advice that helps you make clothing choices that suit your body, lifestyle, preferences, and desired impression. It brings together fit, proportions, color, materials, outfits, and occasions so that your wardrobe becomes easier to use.

In practical terms, a style consultation does not simply tell you what is currently fashionable. It helps you understand why certain clothes feel right, why others remain unworn, and which decisions create a more consistent personal look.

## Style consultation explained simply

Style consultation answers everyday questions such as:

- What clothes feel like me, rather than just looking good on someone else?
- Which cuts and lengths make my outfits feel balanced?
- Why is my wardrobe full, yet getting dressed still feels difficult?
- Which colors, materials, and details should appear more often?
- What do I actually need for work, weekends, events, or a new life phase?

The process turns vague inspiration into useful criteria. Instead of buying another item because it looks appealing in the moment, you learn to ask whether it fits your existing wardrobe, your real schedule, and your desired [personal style](/en/glossar/personal-style/).

## What does a style consultation include?

The exact format varies, but good style advice often considers several connected areas:

| Area                    | What it helps clarify                                                                  |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Personal style          | The style directions, references, and details that feel authentic to you               |
| Fit and proportion      | How garment shape, length, volume, and placement work together on your body            |
| Color                   | Which color relationships support your complexion, contrast level, and desired impact  |
| Wardrobe                | Which pieces work together, which gaps matter, and which purchases are less useful     |
| Lifestyle and occasions | What you truly need for work, daily life, travel, social events, or visibility         |
| Visual impact           | Whether an outfit reads as calm, capable, creative, elegant, approachable, or distinct |

A consultation can include [color consultation](/en/glossar/farbberatung/), but it is broader than color alone. It also connects color choices with silhouettes, fabrics, styling habits, and the real situations in which you wear your clothes.

## Why style consultation matters

Many people do not lack clothes. They lack a system that connects the clothes they own.

A wardrobe may contain beautiful individual pieces, yet still create frustration if the colors compete, the proportions feel inconsistent, or too few items work for everyday life. Style consultation creates more [wardrobe logic](/en/glossar/garderobenlogik/): your clothes begin to support each other rather than requiring a new solution every morning.

This can lead to:

- faster outfit decisions
- fewer impulse purchases and fewer [bad purchases](/en/glossar/fehlkauf/)
- clearer buying criteria
- more outfit combinations from existing pieces
- a more recognizable [signature look](/en/glossar/signature-look/)
- greater confidence in important professional or social situations

## Style consultation is not a makeover

A common misunderstanding is that style consultation means someone replaces your wardrobe or imposes a new identity on you.

A makeover can create a dramatic before and after moment. Style consultation is usually more sustainable when it starts with your own preferences, practical needs, and existing strengths. The aim is not to make everyone look alike or more trend-driven. It is to make your clothing choices more coherent and more personal.

Another misconception is that style advice is only for special occasions. It can be particularly valuable for everyday dressing, when small repeated decisions have the greatest long-term effect.

## Style consultation, style analysis, and image consulting

These terms overlap, but they are not identical.

- **[Style analysis](/en/glossar/stilanalyse/)** identifies recurring patterns in colors, cuts, materials, preferences, and visual impact.
- **Style consultation** uses those findings to make concrete decisions about outfits, shopping, wardrobe planning, and styling habits.
- **[Image consulting](/en/glossar/imageberatung/)** focuses more strongly on how appearance communicates in a professional, social, or public context.
- **[Color consultation](/en/glossar/farbberatung/)** clarifies color direction, undertones, harmony, and contrast, then style consultation places that color knowledge within the wider wardrobe.

In practice, these areas often work best together.

## A real-life style consultation example

Someone may own a tailored blazer, feminine skirts, practical sneakers, and relaxed knitwear, but feel that none of the outfits reflect one clear person. The issue is not necessarily that any piece is wrong.

A style consultation can reveal the connecting thread. Perhaps the person feels most confident in clean lines, soft structure, muted colors, and one distinctive accessory. The blazer may work in a less rigid fabric or a more suitable length. The sneakers may work best with straighter trousers rather than every skirt. The result is not a completely new wardrobe, but a clearer way to combine what is already there.

This is where [proportion](/en/glossar/proportion/), [fit](/en/glossar/passform/), and [impact](/en/glossar/wirkung/) become practical rather than abstract.

## When is style consultation useful?

Style consultation can help at any point, but it is especially useful when:

- your wardrobe no longer reflects your current work, lifestyle, or priorities
- you repeatedly buy clothes that remain unworn
- you have inspiration but cannot translate it into wearable outfits
- you want to build a more recognizable professional presence
- your body or fit preferences have changed
- you want fewer clothes that combine more easily
- you are preparing for a role, event, return to work, move, or other transition

It can also support a [wardrobe check](/en/glossar/kleiderschrank-check/) or [wardrobe planning](/en/glossar/garderobenplanung/) process by showing which pieces genuinely earn their place.

## Practical check: prepare for style advice

Before booking a consultation or reviewing your wardrobe yourself, gather useful evidence rather than relying only on imagined style goals.

- Save five to ten outfits in which you genuinely felt comfortable and confident.
- Identify the clothing you reach for repeatedly, even if it is simple.
- Note the occasions for which getting dressed is difficult.
- Make a list of recent purchases that did not work and ask why.
- Separate your idealized style image from the clothes that make sense in your actual week.
- Choose three words that describe how you want to come across.

The [3-Word Method](/en/glossar/3-worte-methode/) is helpful here. Three clear style words can become a practical filter for new purchases, outfit choices, and alterations.

## ESKYNA perspective

At ESKYNA, style consultation connects clothing with personal presence. Body lines and colors matter, but so do profession, routines, comfort, energy, identity, and the effect you want to create.

A clear style does not have to be loud, expensive, or complicated. It should make you feel more at ease in your clothes and make your appearance feel more aligned with who you are.

Style consultation turns separate clothing choices into a personal system, so you spend less time doubting outfits and more time wearing what feels right.
