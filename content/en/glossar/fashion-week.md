## Definition

Several fashion metropolises and regional markets organize their own fashion weeks. In addition to catwalk shows, this includes showrooms, digital formats, trade fairs, discussions and private appointments.

## In simple words

Fashion Week does not refer to a single global event or exactly seven days. Season, target group and calendar differ for women's, men's, couture and other segments. The fashion shown is often aimed at a later sales season.

## What to pay attention to

- Consider location, season and segment before comparing fashion week trends.
- Distinguish between official calendars, side events and independent shows.
- Evaluate trends only after several collections instead of after a viral single image.

## Common misconceptions

There is no one Fashion Week. The term includes many organized calendars with different roles and target groups.

## ESKYNA note

> Fashion Week is less a single show than a time-packaged conversation in the fashion industry.
