## Definition

The term fashion combines clothing with a positive mood and playful self-expression. It is not a medical method and does not guarantee a measurable release of a specific messenger substance.

## In simple words

For some people, dopamine dressing means bright colors, for others a special material, jewelry or a familiar favorite outfit. The approach is subjective: What matters is not what is generally considered happy, but what feels activating and harmonious to you.

## What to pay attention to

- Choose a personal source of energy instead of randomly combining many loud elements.
- Test whether the color also looks pleasant close to the face.
- Adjust the intensity and area to suit the occasion, form of the day and comfort zone.

## Common misconceptions

Dopamine dressing doesn't mean neon colors from head to toe. Even quiet, personal stimuli can have a positive effect.

## ESKYNA note

> Dopamine dressing doesn't start with what's trendy, but with what visibly makes you happy.
