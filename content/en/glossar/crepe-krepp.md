## Definition

Crepe is not a single fiber, but a family of fabrics and surfaces. The typical structure can be created by heavily twisted yarns, special weaves or finishes; The materials used include silk, wool, viscose and polyester.

## In simple words

Crepe usually appears duller and less smooth than satin. Fine qualities flow softly, firmer versions give dresses, trousers or blazers a clear shape. The slightly irregular surface can make small wrinkles and bumps appear visually calmer.

## What to pay attention to

- Check whether the fabric falls softly or has more stability, as both are sold as crepes.
- Be careful of pulling threads and rough jewelry that can damage delicate surfaces.
- Test whether the fabric wrinkles badly when you sit or sticks to tights.

## Common misconceptions

Crepe does not automatically mean silk. The fiber content determines care, comfort and price.
