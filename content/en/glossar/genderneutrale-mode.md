## Definition

It can include shared collections, flexible sizes, customizable cuts or style codes that are not tied to binary gender roles.

## In simple words

Gender neutral does not mean that all bodies are built the same. A good fit still requires information about dimensions, proportions and cut. Feminine or masculine details can also be offered in a gender-neutral manner and can be worn independently by different people.

## What to pay attention to

- Use specific clothing measurements instead of just the category or size designation.
- Check your shoulders, chest, hips and stride length according to your body.
- Choose the style effect based on your own identity and desired expression, not based on the sales department.

## Common misconceptions

Gender-neutral fashion is not automatically oversized, colorless or androgynous. Neutrality refers to attribution, not to a single optic.
