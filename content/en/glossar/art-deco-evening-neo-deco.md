## Definition

**Art Deco Evening** or **Neo Deco** is an evening style inspired by 1920s and 1930s aesthetics with geometric lines and luxurious textures.

## In simple words

The focus is not costume, but construction. Use line, light, and material contrast with restraint.

## ESKYNA extra: The Deco Code

Use three elements: one clear line, one light catcher, one dark frame.

**ESKYNA line:** Neo Deco is glamour that is constructed, not over-decorated.

## Styling tip

Choose one strong deco accent and keep the rest minimal to avoid costume effect.
