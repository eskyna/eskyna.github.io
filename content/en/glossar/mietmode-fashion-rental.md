## Definition

Offers range from individual rentals for events to subscriptions with regular exchanges. Ownership, cleaning, repairs and logistics usually remain with the provider.

## In simple words

Renting can make sense for rarely needed formal wear, high-quality accessories or experimental looks. However, the environmental impact is not automatically positive: shipping routes, cleaning, packaging, utilization and service life of the parts all play a role. The fit and return period must also work practically.

## What to pay attention to

- Compare rental price, deposit, insurance and possible damage rules.
- Allow enough time for fitting and replacement if necessary.
- Check whether renting replaces a real new purchase or just generates additional consumption.

## Common misconceptions

Fashion rental is not generally sustainable. The benefit depends on frequency of use and the overall operating model.

## ESKYNA note

> Rental fashion is particularly powerful when it fills rare needs and actually replaces possessions.
