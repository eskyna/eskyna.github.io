Shapewear is contouring undergarment designed to smooth transitions, support silhouette lines, and improve garment fall.

## Definition

It works through compression zones, panel construction, and material recovery. Good shapewear stabilizes shape without severely limiting breath or movement. Strength, length, and edge position must match the garment.

## In Simple Words

Shapewear refines lines. It should not fight your mobility.

## Why This Term Matters

Over-compression is the most common mistake. It often creates visible edges, discomfort, and less elegant outcomes.

## Consultation Perspective

In consultation, we match shapewear to fabric weight, fit intention, wear duration, and temperature demands.

## Practical Styling Use

- choose compression level by event duration
- align hem and waist edges with garment structure
- test walking, sitting, and stairs
- prioritize breathable options for long or warm days
- decide after movement, not mirror-only stillness

- test fit and movement
- check behavior in real light
- verify comfort over several hours
- combine with existing wardrobe pieces

## Concrete Example

A fitted dress may look smoother with medium compression than with high compression that creates rolling edges.

## Typical Misunderstanding

Stronger compression does not automatically look slimmer. It can produce harsher transitions.

## Distinction from Similar Terms

Control garments: often stronger structural shaping.

Seamless underwear: light smoothing with less contour effect.

Body suits: may function as shapewear depending on build.

## ESKYNA Key Takeaway

Great shapewear is rarely visible, but its effect is clear.

## Decision Aid Before Purchase or Styling

- is compression realistic for your wear duration
- are edges hidden under your garment
- do you keep natural mobility
- do seams show under light fabric
- can this piece work with multiple outfits

- does it fit your role and context
- does material behavior match your expectation
- can you combine it at least three ways

## When Consultation Helps

Consultation is useful for fitted silhouettes, event dressing, or repeated discomfort with current shapewear.
