## Definition

A **halter bikini**, also called a neckholder bikini, is a bikini top with straps that run upward from the cups and tie or fasten behind the neck. The design leaves the shoulders and upper back open while creating a central, upward line across the chest.

Many halter bikini tops have triangle cups, but the neckline can also appear on underwired, molded, or fuller-coverage swimwear styles. It is defined by the strap placement rather than by one specific cup shape.

## Effect on the Body and Outfit

The upward strap line gives a halter bikini a distinctive, open look. It frames the collarbones, shoulders, and upper chest, making it a popular choice for beachwear and poolside outfits where the shoulder line is part of the visual focus.

Because the straps move inward toward the neck, they can create a more centered visual line through the upper body. For some people, this can make the shoulder area appear more contained. However, the result depends on the width of the straps, the cut of the cups, the print, and the contrast between the bikini and the skin.

A halter bikini can also create gentle lift when the underbust band fits firmly and the neck tie is adjusted well. The tie should refine the cup position rather than take the full weight of the bust. If the straps pull heavily at the neck, the top is unlikely to feel comfortable over a full day.

## Halter Bikini Styles

Common halter bikini variations include:

- **Triangle halter bikini:** Adjustable triangular cups with straps tied around the neck and back. See [Triangle Bikini](/en/glossar/triangel-bikini/) for this classic style.
- **Underwired halter bikini:** A more structured option with underwires or shaped cups for added definition and support.
- **Halter balconette bikini:** A bikini with a wide, open cup line and halter straps. A [Balconette Bikini](/en/glossar/balconette-bikini/) can offer a different support structure from a triangle style.
- **High-neck halter bikini:** A more covered design that still uses neck-based straps or fastening.
- **Halter bandeau bikini:** A bandeau-style top with detachable or fixed straps around the neck. Compare it with a [Bandeau Bikini](/en/glossar/bandeau-bikini/), which may also be worn without straps.

The wider fashion term for this neckline is [Halterneck](/en/glossar/neckholder/). It also appears in dresses, tops, and eveningwear.

## How to Choose a Halter Bikini

Start with the underbust band. It should sit securely against the body without riding upward at the back. A stable band is particularly important because neck straps alone should not be responsible for support.

Choose strap width according to comfort and the level of support you want. Narrow strings create a minimal, adjustable look but may feel less comfortable for extended wear. Wider halter straps distribute pressure over a broader area and can feel more secure.

Cup coverage matters too. Adjustable triangle cups can be moved closer together or farther apart, but they should cover and contain the bust comfortably when you stand, raise your arms, and move naturally. If you prefer a more stable shape, look for lined cups, an underwire, or a firmer underbust construction.

For a coordinated swim look, consider the bikini bottom's waist height and coverage as well. A high-waist bottom creates a different proportion from a low-rise string bottom, even when paired with the same halter top.

## Common Misunderstandings

- **Misunderstanding: Every halter bikini gives strong support.**  
  **Better:** Support comes mainly from the underbust band, cup construction, lining, and closure. A simple string halter bikini may provide light support, while an underwired style can offer more structure.

- **Misunderstanding: Tighter neck straps always create better lift.**  
  **Better:** Over-tightening can cause neck discomfort and distort the cups. Adjust the straps only enough to position the bust comfortably.

- **Misunderstanding: Halter bikinis only suit one shoulder shape.**  
  **Better:** The effect of a halter neckline is individual. Strap width, color, cup shape, and personal comfort matter more than rigid body-shape rules.

- **Misunderstanding: A halter bikini and a triangle bikini are the same thing.**  
  **Better:** Many triangle bikinis use halter ties, but a halter bikini can have several cup shapes, including molded, underwired, or fuller-coverage styles.

## Practical Fit Check

- The underbust band stays level around the body and does not shift upward.
- The cups contain the bust without gaping, cutting in, or exposing more than intended during movement.
- The neck straps feel secure without causing pressure or strain.
- The center and side edges of the cups sit flat against the body where intended.
- The tie or fastening remains secure when wet, especially if the top uses string closures.
- The overall coverage feels right when sitting, swimming, reaching, and walking.

## ESKYNA Key Line

A halter bikini creates an open shoulder line with adjustable lift, but lasting comfort comes from a supportive band and well-positioned cups, not from pulling the neck straps tighter.

## Advisory View

A halter bikini is a useful choice when you want adjustable swimwear with a clear, feminine neckline. Choose the level of structure according to how you plan to wear it: a simple triangle style for light beachwear, or a lined and underwired version when you want more stable support and coverage.
