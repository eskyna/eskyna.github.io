## Definition

**Laced Up** describes a styling trend where lace, ties, ribbons, and lacing details become visible design features.

## In simple words

Lace no longer appears only in expected romantic pieces. The trend looks freshest when lace appears in unexpected placements, on shoes, cuffs, bags, or layered under sporty garments.

## ESKYNA extra: Lace Disruption

Lace feels modern when used as contrast, not as decoration overload.

**ESKYNA line:** Laced Up gets interesting when lace is recontextualized, not romanticized.

## Styling tip

Pair one lace detail with clean basics like denim, a minimal blazer, or plain trousers to keep the look current.
