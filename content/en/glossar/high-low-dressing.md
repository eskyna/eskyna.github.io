## Definition

High-Low Dressing refers to a contemporary styling idea with a clear visual cue. It is less about rigid rules and more about shaping a deliberate style direction.

High-Low Dressing adds a recognizable mood to an outfit through one focused idea.

When used with restraint, High-Low Dressing adds clarity, personality, and a modern edge. The effect is strongest when one element leads and the rest remains understated.
