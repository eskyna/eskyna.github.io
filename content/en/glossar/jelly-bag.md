## Definition

A **Jelly Bag** is a bag made from transparent, semi-transparent, or glossy plastic with a soft, rubber-like look.

## In simple words

The **Jelly Bag** references beachwear, playful Y2K accessories, and summer styling. Its transparent or translucent finish looks lighter and less classic than leather or fabric bags.

Shapes range from shoppers to mini bags and bucket silhouettes. Clear versions stay subtle; brighter colors create stronger statements.

## How to style it

Because the material can reveal the inside, many people combine it with small inner pouches or coordinated inserts. That keeps the look clean while preserving the transparent effect.

## Styling tip

Use it as contrast with clean summer outfits, denim, or monochrome looks. For a more polished effect, pair it with a color-matched inner pouch.
