## Definition

**Scarf Styling** means using scarves as active styling tools, not only as neck accessories.

## In simple words

A single scarf can shift mood, color balance, and silhouette. It can be worn at the waist, in the hair, on a bag handle, over the shoulder, or as a headscarf.

## ESKYNA extra: One scarf, five identities

Business, feminine, creative, dramatic, casual, all from one accessory.

**ESKYNA line:** A scarf is not just an accessory. It is a style switch.

## Styling tip

Pick a scarf color that already appears in your outfit. This makes the accent look intentional and polished.
