## Definition

Leggings, joggers, sneakers, functional jackets, polo shirts or sporty knitted items are combined in such a way that comfort and urban everyday suitability come together.

## In simple words

Not every athleisure item is made for intensive sports, and not every functional clothing automatically looks well-groomed in everyday life. A harmonious look is created through clean materials, controlled proportions and conscious breaks, such as leggings with a long coat or track pants with a fine knit.

## What to pay attention to

- Distinguish between real performance functions and purely sporty looks.
- Look for opaque fabrics, sturdy cuffs and well-maintained surfaces.
- Combine at most a few very sporty signals if you want the look to appear more adult.

## Common misconceptions

Athleisure is not just sportswear that happens to be worn in everyday life. Styling and context make the difference.

## ESKYNA note

> Athleisure is convincing when comfort is visibly planned and not just conveniently chosen.
