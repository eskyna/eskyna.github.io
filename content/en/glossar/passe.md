## Definition

Fits often fit on the shoulders and upper back, waist or hips. They can be straight, curved or tapered and can be combined with pleats, gathers or added width underneath.

## In simple words

The back yoke of a shirt stabilizes the shoulder area and influences the fit. A hip yoke on a skirt or trousers can replace the waistband and make the waist look calmer. Visible seams, contrasting fabrics or special shapes also make the yoke a design element.

## What to pay attention to

- Check whether the yoke on the shoulders or hips lies smoothly and does not stick out.
- Pay attention to where wrinkles start and whether they create unwanted volume.
- Use horizontal yokes consciously because they visually divide the body line.

## Common misconceptions

A yoke is not just any dividing seam. It is its own, shaping pattern.

## ESKYNA mnemonic

> The yoke organizes fabric and proportions - it supports, distributes and shapes at the same time.
