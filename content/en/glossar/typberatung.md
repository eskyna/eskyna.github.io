## Definition

**Personal Type Consultation**: Personal Type Consultation is a holistic consultation about color, style, proportion, presence, and wardrobe. It shows which visual decisions support your personality and which combinations make you feel more confident.

## In simple words

The term is often used as an umbrella. It includes questions such as: Which colors suit me? Which cuts work? Which style direction feels right? How do I come across in certain outfits? A good consultation connects the answers.

## Why Personal Type Consultation matters

Personal Type Consultation helps when you face many individual decisions. Color alone does not solve every style problem, and a good cut looks different when the color is off. The connection between the elements makes clothing usable.

## ESKYNA perspective

At ESKYNA, the focus is not the type, but the person. I use type characteristics as orientation, not as rigid labels. The goal is that you can make better decisions in everyday life.

## How to use this idea

- Clarify which questions truly bother you.
- Look at color, cut, and impact together.
- Collect examples from your real everyday life.
- Make sure recommendations can be implemented practically.

## Everyday example

In Personal Type Consultation, the first question may be: "What type am I?" The better follow-up question is: which colors, cuts, and effects make me feel secure? A category then becomes a practical tool for clothing, hair, glasses, and wardrobe decisions.

## When consultation helps

Consultation is useful when you need an entry point and do not yet know whether color, cut, or style direction is the main issue. A good Personal Type Consultation sorts the most important levers and shows which next step will have the greatest benefit.

## Common misunderstanding

Personal Type Consultation is often confused with fixed categories. Categories can help, but they should never become more important than your life, taste, and development.

## Difference from related terms

Personal Type Consultation is broader than color consultation and often less specific than a fully developed style consultation. It can be a useful starting point when the result is translated into wardrobe decisions.

## Real consulting situation

Personal type consultation is useful when it does not stop at categories. A type is not a cage but a starting point: which lines, colors, materials, and details strengthen you without pushing you into a role that is not yours?

## Mini case: from doubt to decision

A mini case: a person is often classified as “classic” but feels too correct in that look. The consultation shows that the clear line is right, but softer materials and one modern detail are needed. Order remains, yet the look feels more alive.

## Decision filter before your next purchase

Answer the question “What suits me?” more personally than rigid type boxes can. Use this short filter before you buy, remove pieces, or plan an outfit for an important situation.

- Use type terms as orientation, not as a final diagnosis.
- Always consider body, face, lifestyle, and desired impact together.
- Notice whether a recommendation strengthens you or only makes you look proper.
- Translate type knowledge into concrete shopping and styling rules.

## Signs this topic needs support

Support becomes useful when you collect a lot of inspiration but it does not turn into reliable decisions. One sign is copying outfits and still not feeling like yourself in them. In consultation, no style is imposed on you. The work reveals which lines, colors, and details can carry your personality repeatedly.
