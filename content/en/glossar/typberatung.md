## Definition

**Personal Type Consultation** is a holistic form of style advice that connects color, style, proportions, presence, and wardrobe needs. Its purpose is to show which visual choices support your personality, everyday life, and desired impact.

Rather than answering only one question, such as “Which colors suit me?”, it looks at how several factors work together. A color can be harmonious but feel too quiet for your goals. A cut can fit well but not express the image you want to communicate. Personal Type Consultation brings these elements into one usable framework.

## Personal Type Consultation explained simply

In simple terms, Personal Type Consultation helps you understand what makes an outfit feel like _you_.

It may include questions such as:

- Which colors work well with my natural coloring and the effect I want to create?
- Which silhouettes and proportions feel balanced, comfortable, and convincing?
- Which materials, details, and styling choices reflect my personality?
- How do I want to come across at work, socially, or in more visible situations?
- Which existing clothes already support my style direction?

A useful consultation does not produce a list of prohibitions. It creates a clearer basis for choosing clothes, combining outfits, and buying with more intention.

## What does a Personal Type Consultation include?

The exact focus can vary. Some consultations begin with [Color Consultation](/en/glossar/farbberatung/), while others begin with fit, style direction, or a current wardrobe challenge. Common areas include:

### Color and personal coloring

Color is often an important starting point. It can clarify temperature, brightness, saturation, and contrast, helping you understand why some shades make your features appear clearer while others can feel disconnected.

Still, color is only one part of the picture. Your preferred [Color Palette](/en/glossar/farbpalette/), lifestyle, and desired [Impact](/en/glossar/wirkung/) also matter.

### Style direction and personal expression

A consultation can help name the visual language that feels most natural to you. You may prefer clear and classic lines, relaxed and understated outfits, feminine detail, modern contrast, or a mix of several directions.

This is closely connected to [Personal Style](/en/glossar/personal-style/). The goal is not to copy a trend or perform a fixed identity. It is to identify recurring qualities that make your clothing feel coherent.

### Proportions, cut, and fit

Personal Type Consultation can also address how lengths, volume, waist placement, shoulders, necklines, and fabric drape shape an outfit. This is not about correcting the body. It is about understanding how [Proportion](/en/glossar/proportion/), [Silhouette](/en/glossar/silhouette/), and [Fit](/en/glossar/passform/) influence comfort and visual balance.

### Presence and desired impression

Clothing communicates before you speak. Depending on the situation, you may want to appear calm, approachable, authoritative, creative, polished, or relaxed. A consultation considers this connection between clothing and [Presence](/en/glossar/praesenz/).

That makes the process particularly useful when you are navigating a new role, more visible work, a change in lifestyle, or a wardrobe that no longer feels aligned with who you are.

## Why Personal Type Consultation matters

Many people already know individual style rules but still struggle to get dressed. They may own colors they like, clothes that technically fit, and saved outfit inspiration, yet the wardrobe does not feel connected.

Personal Type Consultation helps because it considers the relationship between the individual parts:

- Color affects freshness, contrast, and mood.
- Cut affects movement, shape, and balance.
- Materials and details affect how refined, relaxed, soft, or structured an outfit feels.
- Styling affects whether the final look expresses your intended message.
- Lifestyle determines whether recommendations are actually wearable.

When these elements support one another, outfit decisions usually become faster and more reliable. This can also reduce impulsive purchases and make [Wardrobe Planning](/en/glossar/garderobenplanung/) more focused.

## Personal Type Consultation is not a fixed label

A common misunderstanding is that Personal Type Consultation assigns someone a permanent category that determines every future choice.

Type descriptions can be useful as a shared language. They can help identify recurring strengths or explain why certain choices work. But they should remain flexible. Personal style develops, daily needs change, and people may want different forms of expression in different contexts.

A recommendation is valuable when it gives you more options and clearer choices, not when it makes you afraid of wearing something you enjoy.

## Personal Type Consultation vs. related services

### Personal Type Consultation vs. Color Consultation

[Color Consultation](/en/glossar/farbberatung/) focuses on the colors that harmonize with your coloring and support a desired effect. Personal Type Consultation is broader because it can include color alongside style, proportions, image, and wardrobe use.

### Personal Type Consultation vs. Style Consultation

[Style Consultation](/en/glossar/stilberatung/) is often more focused on personal style direction, outfit building, and practical wardrobe decisions. Personal Type Consultation may serve as a broad introduction or assessment before deeper style work begins.

### Personal Type Consultation vs. Style Analysis

A [Style Analysis](/en/glossar/stilanalyse/) examines what is already visible in your preferences, proportions, lifestyle, and clothing patterns. Personal Type Consultation may use this analysis to create practical recommendations and next steps.

### Personal Type Consultation vs. Image Consulting

[Image Consulting](/en/glossar/imageberatung/) has a stronger focus on how appearance supports communication, professional roles, and public perception. Personal Type Consultation can overlap with this area when desired impact and presence are central concerns.

## Everyday example: from a type label to a useful wardrobe decision

Someone may be described as “classic” because they look good in clear lines, tailored pieces, and understated colors. Yet they may feel overly formal or invisible when they dress in a strictly classic way.

A useful Personal Type Consultation would not simply repeat the label. It might identify that the person benefits from structured shapes but needs softer textures, a more modern shoe, or a stronger accent color to feel authentic.

The result is not “dress classic.” It is a practical style direction: use clean lines as a foundation, add movement or texture, and include one contemporary detail that brings the look to life.

This kind of translation is what turns a broad type concept into a wardrobe that works.

## Practical check: how to use the idea yourself

Before buying a new piece or planning an important outfit, ask:

- Does this color support my features and the mood I want to create?
- Does the cut give me enough comfort and ease of movement?
- Does the proportion feel balanced with the rest of my outfit?
- Does this piece fit my real life, rather than only an imagined version of it?
- Can I combine it with clothes I already wear often?
- Does it feel recognizably like me?

If several answers are unclear, it may be more helpful to seek guidance than to keep collecting isolated style rules.

## When Personal Type Consultation is especially helpful

Personal Type Consultation can be valuable when:

- you feel unsure whether color, fit, or style direction is the main issue
- your wardrobe contains many clothes but few reliable outfits
- your lifestyle, work role, body, or confidence has changed
- you like inspiration on others but cannot translate it into your own wardrobe
- you want a more recognizable [Signature Look](/en/glossar/signature-look/)
- you are tired of buying pieces that look promising but remain unworn

It can provide a clear starting point, particularly when your style questions feel connected but difficult to sort alone.

## ESKYNA perspective

At ESKYNA, the focus is not on fitting a person into a type. Type characteristics can be useful orientation, but they are never more important than the individual person.

The aim is to connect color, style, proportions, personality, and everyday requirements in a way that supports confident decisions. You should be able to understand why a recommendation works and use it independently in your wardrobe.
