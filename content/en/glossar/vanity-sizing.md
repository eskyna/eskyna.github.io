## Definition

This allows a person to wear a lower number on one brand than on another. Sizes differ depending on country, target group, cut and brand system; Vanity sizing increases these deviations.

## In simple words

The number in the label is therefore not a reliable body assessment or a quality feature. For online purchases, specific clothing measurements, material stretch and empirical values ​​are more helpful. Even within the same brand, collections can vary.

## What to pay attention to

- Use measurement tables and compare them with your own well-fitting item of clothing.
- Don't automatically order the same number from every brand or cut.
- Remove the emotional meaning from size and only evaluate fit, comfort and impact.

## Common misconceptions

Vanity sizing does not mean that there is a single right size and all other brands are wrong. Clothing sizes are generally only standardized to a limited extent.
