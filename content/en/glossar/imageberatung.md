## Definition

**Image Consulting**: Image consulting looks at how you are perceived and whether this perception matches your role, personality, and goals. Clothing is an important part, but not the only one. Color, body language, occasion, tone, and recognition also shape the impression.

## In simple words

While style consultation asks what suits you, image consulting also asks what your appearance communicates. Does your look feel approachable, clear, creative, trustworthy, confident, or distant? Which signals should become stronger, and which ones may be appearing unintentionally?

## Why Image Consulting matters

Image matters when you are visible: at work, in photos, on stage, in client meetings, while networking, or while building a personal brand. A coherent image does not make you artificial. It reduces the gap between who you are and what people notice first.

## ESKYNA perspective

At ESKYNA, image consulting is not about creating a mask. I look at the impact that is already present and how it can become clearer. Sometimes a look needs more calm, sometimes more color, sometimes a more precise silhouette or a wardrobe that shows competence without becoming hard.

## How to use this idea

- Define first what you want to be visible for.
- Check whether your clothes support that message.
- Look for consistency in photos, appointments, and everyday situations.
- Decide which details are intentional and which still feel random.

## Everyday example

A typical example is a professional presence that is strong in expertise but visually unclear. On LinkedIn, in presentations, or in client meetings, a look can quickly feel too private, too strict, or too random. Image consulting does not ask what is fashionable. It asks which message your appearance sends and whether it supports your role.

## When consultation helps

Consultation becomes especially valuable when visibility increases. A new role, self-employment, public speaking, applications, photo shoots, or media presence change the demands on clothing. A nice outfit is no longer enough. The look needs to make competence visible and still feel human.

## Common misunderstanding

Image consulting does not mean pretending to be someone else. It shows which signals already exist and how you can guide them more consciously. Authentic impact appears when inner self-image and outer impression move closer together.

## Difference from related terms

Image consulting differs from personal branding because it works more directly with visible appearance. It differs from style consultation because it considers the communicative effect of a look even more explicitly.

## Real consulting situation

Image consulting often becomes important when a role has grown: a new leadership position, self-employment, stage work, photos, or greater visibility. Clothing then needs to do more than look nice. It has to support the message people receive in the first seconds.

## Mini case: from doubt to decision

A typical mini case: an expert is highly competent, yet her outfits look too soft and lose authority in photos. The solution is not to make everything stricter. Clearer lines, fewer tiny prints, and one recognizable accent color can keep warmth while strengthening professional impact.

## Decision filter before your next purchase

Align visible impact so competence, personality, and role communicate the same message. Use this short filter before you buy, remove pieces, or plan an outfit for an important situation.

- Compare your desired image with three current photos or video stills.
- Check whether your clothing supports your role or weakens it unconsciously.
- Think about long-distance impact: lines, contrast, and recognition matter more than tiny details.
- Define three qualities your presence should communicate immediately.

## Signs this topic needs support

Support becomes useful when clothing no longer needs to work only privately but also carries professional impact, visibility, or responsibility. The more important an appointment, stage, or new role becomes, the more the message of your look matters. A clear outside view prevents the outfit from feeling too strict, too random, or too restrained.
