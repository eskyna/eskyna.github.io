## Definition

**Image consulting** is a form of consultation that examines how a person is perceived and how closely that perception matches their personality, role, values, and goals. It considers clothing, color, fit, grooming, body language, and context as parts of one visible message.

In simple terms, image consulting helps reduce the gap between who you are, what you want to be known for, and what people notice at first glance. It is not about inventing a persona. It is about making your existing strengths more visible and coherent.

## What does image consulting look at?

A personal image is shaped by more than whether an outfit is fashionable or flattering. Image consulting asks what your appearance communicates before a conversation even begins.

Relevant areas can include:

- your [personal style](/en/glossar/personal-style/) and how recognisable it feels
- color, contrast, and the emotional tone of an outfit
- fit, silhouette, and the degree of structure in clothing
- the balance between approachability, authority, creativity, and calm
- visual consistency across everyday life, meetings, presentations, photos, and online profiles
- the expectations of a specific role, industry, audience, or occasion

A relaxed knit and sneakers may communicate accessibility and ease. A sharply tailored jacket may create more structure and authority. Neither is inherently better. The useful choice depends on the message, the situation, and the person wearing it.

## Image consulting versus style consulting

[Style consultation](/en/glossar/stilberatung/) helps clarify what suits you in terms of color, proportions, fit, wardrobe needs, and style direction. Image consulting builds on this work by asking an additional question: **What does the finished appearance communicate?**

For example, someone may know that a certain color and cut suit them, yet still feel visually unclear in a leadership role or on camera. Image consulting looks at the wider effect. It considers whether the outfit supports the person’s role, message, and desired [presence](/en/glossar/praesenz/).

## Why image consulting matters

Image matters most when people form impressions quickly or when your role carries responsibility. This can include job interviews, client meetings, networking, presentations, media appearances, professional photography, or building a public-facing business.

A coherent image does not make someone artificial. It can make communication easier because the visible signals and the spoken message support each other. When competence, personality, and appearance point in the same direction, people often feel clearer and more credible.

This is closely connected to [personal branding](/en/glossar/personal-branding/), but image consulting is more directly concerned with how that brand is expressed through your visible appearance.

## Image consulting in everyday life

You do not need a public role to benefit from a more intentional image. Everyday image consulting can be as practical as choosing a wardrobe that feels like you, works for your life, and removes uncertainty from getting dressed.

Ask yourself:

- How do I want to feel when I enter a room?
- Which three qualities should people notice first?
- Do my usual outfits support those qualities?
- Where does my appearance feel inconsistent with my role or personality?
- Which clothes make me feel visible without feeling disguised?

These questions can guide a more focused [wardrobe strategy](/en/glossar/garderobenstrategie/) and help avoid purchases that are attractive in isolation but do not support your real life.

## Mini case: strengthening authority without losing warmth

Imagine an experienced consultant whose expertise is clear in conversation, but whose photos and meeting outfits appear visually unfocused. The goal is not to make her look severe or unlike herself.

A useful approach could be to choose clearer jacket shapes, reduce very small prints, refine fit, and repeat one recognisable accent color. These changes can preserve warmth while making the overall impression more focused, confident, and professional.

This is the core of image consulting: not a costume, but an outward expression that supports what is already there.

## Common misunderstandings

- **Image consulting means pretending to be someone else.**  
  Effective image consulting does the opposite. It identifies which qualities are already authentic and makes them easier to recognise.

- **It is only about expensive clothing.**  
  Price alone does not create impact. Fit, condition, proportion, color, and consistency often matter more than a label.

- **A professional image must be strict or formal.**  
  Professionalism can look structured, creative, calm, modern, warm, or understated. The right expression depends on your role and context.

- **You need a completely new wardrobe.**  
  Often, a clearer image starts with editing, combining, and prioritising what you already own more intentionally.

## Practical image check

Before an important appointment, presentation, photo shoot, or meeting, use this short check:

1. **Name the situation.** What is the setting, and what does it require?
2. **Define your intended impact.** Choose three words, such as clear, warm, capable, modern, calm, or creative.
3. **Assess the whole look.** Check color, fit, shoes, accessories, and grooming together rather than as separate items.
4. **Look at distance and camera effect.** Strong lines, contrast, and a clear focal point usually register more quickly than small details.
5. **Check recognition.** Does the outfit still feel like you and support your [image](/en/glossar/image/) over time?

## When image consulting is especially useful

Image consulting can be valuable when your visibility or responsibilities change, for example:

- starting a new leadership role
- becoming self-employed
- preparing for interviews or high-stakes meetings
- speaking on stage or appearing on video
- planning professional portraits or a personal brand shoot
- returning to work after a life transition
- creating a more coherent professional wardrobe

For leaders and public-facing professionals, this often overlaps with [executive presence](/en/glossar/executive-presence/): the ability to communicate clarity, credibility, and confidence through both behaviour and appearance.

## ESKYNA perspective

At ESKYNA, image consulting is not about creating a mask or following a fixed set of rules. It begins with the impact that is already present and asks how that impact can become clearer.

Sometimes the answer is more calm. Sometimes it is stronger color, a more precise silhouette, more intentional contrast, or fewer random elements. The goal is a visual language that feels authentic, supports your goals, and gives you greater confidence in visible situations.
