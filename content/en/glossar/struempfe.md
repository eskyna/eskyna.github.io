## Definition

**Stockings** are separate hosiery pieces that cover the foot and part of the leg. They commonly end below the knee, at the knee, or on the thigh. Unlike tights, stockings are not connected by a panty section.

The word can describe several styles, from practical knee-high stockings worn under trousers to sheer thigh-high styles chosen for dresses, skirts, or lingerie looks. Their appearance depends on length, opacity, finish, top-band construction, and color.

## Stockings Explained Simply

Stockings create a clean, continuous leg line while allowing more flexibility than tights. Because they are separate, they can feel cooler and easier to wear under fitted clothing, especially when a full panty section is not wanted.

Knee-high stockings are often a discreet everyday choice with trousers, long skirts, and boots. Thigh-high stockings are more visible and are often worn with dresses, skirts, or occasionwear. They may be held up with a silicone band, as with [stay-up stockings](/en/glossar/halterlose-struempfe/), or attached to a [garter belt and garters](/en/glossar/strapsguertel/).

Stockings can be sheer, matte, glossy, opaque, patterned, or textured. The [denier](/en/glossar/denier/) indicates the yarn density and gives a useful guide to coverage. Lower denier styles generally look more transparent, while higher denier hosiery provides a denser, more covered effect.

## Effect on an Outfit

Stockings influence the polish, mood, and formality of an outfit.

- **Sheer nude stockings** can create an even-looking leg finish while remaining subtle.
- **Sheer black stockings** add contrast, definition, and a more dressed-up effect.
- **Opaque stockings** bring stronger coverage and work well in cooler weather or with heavier fabrics.
- **Seamed or patterned styles** become a deliberate focal point and can add a vintage, sensual, or fashion-forward detail.
- **Knee-high stockings** are often practical and understated, especially with tailoring and boots.

The right finish matters. Matte hosiery tends to look quieter and more natural, while sheen catches the light and makes the legs more noticeable. Consider the outfit’s [dress code](/en/glossar/dresscode/), the season, and how much visual emphasis you want on the legs.

## How to Wear Stockings

Choose stocking length according to the outfit and your comfort.

### With dresses and skirts

For shorter hemlines, thigh-high stockings need a top band that remains covered during walking and sitting. A seated check is especially useful before leaving home. If you prefer a secure traditional construction, stockings worn with a garter belt offer adjustable placement.

With midi and maxi skirts, knee-high or thigh-high stockings can work well because the top edge remains hidden. Match the hosiery’s visual weight to the fabric. Fine sheer stockings suit fluid dresses and lighter eveningwear, while more opaque styles balance wool, denim, knitwear, and structured fabrics.

### With trousers and boots

Knee-high stockings are a practical choice under [trousers](/en/glossar/hose/) and boots. They prevent direct friction between the foot and shoe, add a light layer of warmth, and avoid the bulk of full tights.

Avoid a top edge that feels overly tight or leaves deep marks. A secure stocking should stay in place without pinching, rolling, or restricting circulation.

### With shoes

Stockings should sit smoothly in the shoe, particularly at the toe and heel. Tight shoes can create pressure, wrinkles, or rapid wear. Closed pumps, loafers, and boots are usually easy pairings, while open shoes require a clean toe finish and an intentional styling choice.

## Common Misunderstandings

- **Misunderstanding: Stockings and tights are the same thing.**  
  **Better:** Stockings cover each leg separately, while tights are joined at the hips through a panty section.

- **Misunderstanding: All thigh-high stockings stay up on their own.**  
  **Better:** Some require a garter belt. Only stay-up styles are designed with grip bands intended to hold them in place without suspenders.

- **Misunderstanding: Higher denier always means better quality.**  
  **Better:** Denier mainly describes yarn density. Durability also depends on fiber quality, construction, fit, and how the hosiery is cared for.

- **Misunderstanding: Nude stockings should always match the skin exactly.**  
  **Better:** A close match can look discreet, but the best choice also depends on undertone, transparency, lighting, and the overall outfit.

## Practical Check Before Wearing Stockings

- Check that the toe area is smooth and not pulling tightly.
- Make sure the heel sits in the correct place rather than twisting under the foot.
- Walk, sit, and bend to see whether thigh-high stockings remain covered by the hem.
- Confirm that the top band stays secure without rolling or digging in.
- Choose opacity that suits the season, outfit fabric, and occasion.
- Keep nails, rings, and rough shoe interiors away from delicate sheer hosiery to reduce snagging.

## Care Tips

Hand washing is often the gentlest option for delicate stockings. Use mild detergent, avoid twisting the fabric, and let them air dry away from direct heat. Store sheer pairs separately from rough fabrics, hooks, and jewelry that could catch the knit.

For everyday use, keeping an extra pair in a bag or drawer can be helpful, especially when wearing fine denier stockings to work or an event.
