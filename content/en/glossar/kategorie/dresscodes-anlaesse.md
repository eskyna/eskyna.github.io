## What this category covers

This category explains dress codes, invitations, professional settings, and special occasions. It helps you understand terms such as Black Tie, Cocktail Attire, Business Formal, and Smart Casual without losing your own style.

## How to use these terms

Start with the situation: venue, time of day, host, industry, and expected formality. Then check which pieces you already own can be styled more polished, festive, or understated.

## Practical focus

The strongest occasion looks balance appropriateness with personality. Shoes, bag, outer layer, and accessories should support the same level of formality.
