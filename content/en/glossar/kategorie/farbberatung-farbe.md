## What this category covers

This category explains color type, undertone, contrast, temperature, palette, and color harmony. It helps you understand why some shades make the face look clearer while others feel heavy or disconnected.

## How to use these terms

Test color in context: near your face, with jewelry, glasses, make-up, and existing wardrobe pieces. The goal is not to ban colors, but to choose the versions that support your presence.

## Practical focus

Comparing similar shades is especially useful. Warm beige, cool taupe, and soft greige may look close on a hanger but send very different signals when worn.
