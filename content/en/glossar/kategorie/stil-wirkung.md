## What this category covers

This category is about presence, image, personal style, recognizability, and visual messages. It helps you understand clothing as communication, not just as outfits.

## How to use these terms

First define the impression you want to create: calm, competent, creative, approachable, strong, elegant, unconventional, or warm. Then check whether color, cut, material, and detail all support that direction.

## Practical focus

Strong impact does not have to be loud. Often it comes from clarity, repetition, and one detail that acts like a thread through your style.
