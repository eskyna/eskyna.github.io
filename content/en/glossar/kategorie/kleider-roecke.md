## What this category covers

Dresses and skirts work through length, silhouette, fabric, movement, and waist placement. This category explains shapes such as A-line, slip dress, pencil skirt, midi length, and wrap dress in practical styling terms.

## How to use these terms

Always test a dress or skirt with the shoes you would actually wear. The connection between hemline, heel height, and top layer often decides whether the proportions feel balanced.

## Practical focus

Movement matters. Sitting, walking, taking stairs, and everyday comfort are just as important as the first mirror impression.
