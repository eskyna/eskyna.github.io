## What this category covers

The color lexicon collects individual shades and color families, from navy and terracotta to cream, camel, petrol, and off-white. It helps you name colors more precisely and compare similar nuances.

## How to use these terms

Do not judge a color by name alone. Test the actual fabric near the face, as a large garment, and as a small accent. The same shade can feel soft in one material and too strong in another.

## Practical focus

Pay attention to temperature, brightness, saturation, and surface. Shine, texture, and transparency can change the visual effect of a color dramatically.
