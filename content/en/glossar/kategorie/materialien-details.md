## What this category covers

This category explains fabrics, surfaces, construction, finishes, and small details that influence quality and style impact. Material decides whether a look feels soft, structured, polished, casual, festive, or functional.

## How to use these terms

Do not judge material only visually. Feel the hand, observe drape and creasing, check care instructions, and ask whether the detail supports several outfits.

## Practical focus

Two similar cuts can look completely different because of fabric and finishing. This is often the difference between a piece that looks good in store and a piece that truly gets worn.
