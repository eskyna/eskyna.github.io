## What this category covers

Shoes influence posture, leg line, formality, comfort, and the mood of an outfit. This category explains shoe shapes, heel types, sole weight, boot heights, and styling details.

## How to use these terms

Always check shoes with the trousers, skirt, or dress length you plan to wear. The meeting point between hem and shoe often changes the whole proportion.

## Practical focus

Comfort is part of impact. A shoe that makes you walk uncertainly weakens even a beautiful outfit. Style and movement should support each other.
