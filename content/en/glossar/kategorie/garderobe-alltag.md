## What this category covers

This category connects style knowledge with everyday wardrobe decisions: shopping criteria, wardrobe gaps, capsule wardrobe, favorite pieces, outfit formulas, and long-term wardrobe logic.

## How to use these terms

Ask what role each term plays in your real life. Do you need it for work, travel, visibility, comfort, events, or relaxed everyday dressing? This turns inspiration into practical choices.

## Practical focus

A strong wardrobe is not the fullest wardrobe. It is a wardrobe where pieces repeat, combine easily, and still feel like you.
