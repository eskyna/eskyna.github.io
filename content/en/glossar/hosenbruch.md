## Definition

Depending on the length and width, one speaks of no, slight, half or full break. The terms describe varying amounts of visible wrinkling across the shoe.

## In simple words

Slim, modern trousers are often worn with little or no breakage; more classic or additional models may have a heavier base. Too much length can compress the drape and appear untidy, while a hem that is too short will lose the desired degree of formality.

## What to pay attention to

- Assess the length using the specific shoe model and standing upright.
- Check front and back: The hem should not drag on the floor.
- Match the break to the leg width, fabric weight and occasion.

## Common misconceptions

The trouser break is not a damaged or permanently ironed fold. It is created by the controlled impact of the hem on the shoe.

## ESKYNA note

> The trouser fold is the final line of a pair of trousers - a few centimeters decide between clear, classic and compressed.
