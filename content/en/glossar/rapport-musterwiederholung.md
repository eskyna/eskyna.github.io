## Definition

A repeat has a certain height and width. When cutting, it is important to take into account how stripes, checks, flowers or graphic motifs will continue along seams, pockets and fasteners.

## In simple words

Large repeats require more fabric because pattern pieces have to be moved to fit. A neatly coordinated pattern makes clothing appear more high-quality; Unintentional interruptions or poorly placed motifs are particularly noticeable in the middle of the body. With irregular prints, repetition is less strict, but placement remains important.

## What to pay attention to

- Check side, shoulder and center front seams for matching pattern connections.
- Pay attention to the position of large motifs on the chest, buttocks and crotch.
- Judge whether pockets continue the pattern or are deliberately placed as a contrast.

## Common misconceptions

Rapport does not mean the entire visible pattern, but rather its recurring basic unit.
