## Definition

Depending on the model, one or both sides of the shoe are open. The toe area and heel typically remain closed and the heel can be flat to high.

## In simple words

The cut-out side line makes the foot appear lighter and longer. At the same time, there is a lack of lateral support, which is why fit and heel guidance are particularly important. If the material is soft, the shoe may gape; If there are hard edges, it can cut into the arch of the foot.

## What to pay attention to

- Take a few steps and check whether the heel is secure.
- Make sure that the cut edge does not stick out or pinch.
- Test the shoe later in the day when your feet have a little more volume.

## Common misconceptions

D'Orsay does not specify a specific heel height. The characteristic feature is the shape that is open to the sides.

## ESKYNA note

> D'Orsay pumps appear particularly light due to their lack of side surface - which is why the fit has to be precise.
