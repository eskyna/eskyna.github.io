## Definition

A **halterneck**, also called a **halter neckline**, is a neckline in which straps, ties, or shaped fabric rise to the neck instead of sitting on the shoulders. The design is common in tops, dresses, swimwear, and jumpsuits.

A halterneck may tie behind the neck, fasten with a clasp, or continue into a high neck section. It is the neck-based support that defines the cut, not whether the front is high, low, fitted, or flowing.

## How a halterneck looks and feels

By leaving the shoulders open, a halterneck directs attention upward toward the neck, collarbone, shoulders, and upper back. Its upward diagonal lines can create a focused, expressive frame around the upper body. The overall effect depends greatly on fabric, width of straps, and neckline shape.

A narrow tie halterneck can feel summery, sensual, or relaxed. A wide, sculpted halterneck in a structured fabric can appear clean and modern. In eveningwear, a halterneck often adds an elegant exposed-back detail without requiring a deep front neckline.

The design also has a practical side: much of the garment's weight may rest at the neck. A light halterneck top can feel effortless, while a heavier dress or a style with narrow ties may become uncomfortable over several hours.

## Why halternecks matter in styling

Halternecks strongly influence the visible shoulder line and neckline area. They can be useful when you want to make the upper body the focal point of an outfit, especially with simple trousers, skirts, or minimal accessories.

They also affect the balance of an outfit. Since the neckline is visually present, it often works best with a considered amount of detail elsewhere. A statement earring, an open back, or a defined waist can complement the cut, while several competing details may make the look feel busy.

For a more complete view of how necklines shape visual balance, see [Neckline Effect](/en/glossar/ausschnittwirkung/) and [Shoulder Line](/en/glossar/schulterlinie/).

## Halterneck tops, dresses, and swimwear

### Halterneck top

A halterneck top exposes the shoulders and can range from casual jersey styles to polished woven blouses. It is often paired with jeans, tailored trousers, or a skirt to contrast its open upper line with a more grounded lower half.

### Halterneck dress

A halterneck dress can feel relaxed, festive, or formal depending on its length, material, and construction. A flowing maxi version creates movement, while a close-fitting dress makes the neckline and upper back more central to the look.

### Halterneck swimwear

In swimwear, the halterneck provides a recognizable shoulder-baring shape. Its comfort and support depend on strap width, construction, and how the garment fits across the bust and neck.

## What to pay attention to before buying

- **Check neck comfort:** Wear the piece for a few minutes rather than judging only in the fitting room mirror. The fastening should not pull or press uncomfortably.
- **Assess support:** Look at the bust area, side coverage, armholes, and closure. A halterneck does not automatically provide enough support for every wearer or occasion.
- **Test movement:** Raise your arms, turn, sit, and bend slightly. This helps reveal whether the neckline shifts or exposes more than intended.
- **Plan underwear:** Visible conventional straps can disrupt the design. Consider integrated cups, a suitable [strapless bra](/en/glossar/traegerloser-bh/), an [adhesive bra](/en/glossar/klebe-bh/), or another solution that suits the garment.
- **Consider the back:** If the garment has an open back, check whether the cut feels secure and appropriate for the setting.

## Common misconceptions about halternecks

- **“A halterneck always has a deep neckline.”**  
  Not necessarily. A halterneck can have a high, modest, keyhole, V-shaped, or plunging front. The support at the neck is what matters.

- **“A halterneck is always backless.”**  
  No. Many halterneck designs expose the upper back, but some have a fully covered back.

- **“All halternecks work with the same bra.”**  
  No. The best underwear depends on the neckline, back opening, fabric opacity, and level of support required. Good [bra fit](/en/glossar/bh-passform/) remains important even when underwear is not visible.

- **“Halternecks suit only evening looks.”**  
  They appear in casual, sporty, beach, and formal wardrobes alike. Fabric and styling determine the occasion.

## Styling a halterneck

Keep the neckline area intentional. Hair worn up can highlight the neck and shoulder line, while loose hair can soften the exposed area. Earrings and a [back necklace](/en/glossar/rueckenkette/) can add detail, but the neckline itself may already be the focal point.

For everyday outfits, combine a simple halterneck top with straight-leg jeans, wide-leg trousers, or an A-line skirt. For an occasion look, choose refined materials such as satin, crepe, or velvet and balance the exposed shoulders with a more covered skirt length or a clean outer layer.
