## Definition

Straps, ribbons or a raised piece of fabric run towards the neck and replace classic shoulder straps. Halternecks are available on tops, dresses, swimwear and jumpsuits.

## In simple words

The lines lead the eye diagonally upwards and emphasize the shoulders, neck and upper back. Depending on the width, the cut can look sporty, glamorous or minimalist. However, the weight of the garment is placed more heavily on the neck, and classic bra straps are often left visible.

## What to pay attention to

- Check whether the neck closure remains comfortable even after a long time.
- Test the armholes for visibility and sufficient support.
- Before purchasing, clarify which underwear or integrated support works.

## Common misconceptions

Halter does not automatically mean a deep neckline. The fastening in the neck is characteristic.

## ESKYNA mnemonic

> The halterneck opens the shoulder line and concentrates support in the neck - visually clear, physically noticeable.
