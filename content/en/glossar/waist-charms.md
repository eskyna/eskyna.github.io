## Definition

**Waist Charms** are small decorative pendants attached to belts, belt loops, chains, or directly around the waist.

## In simple words

**Waist Charms** move the charm trend from bags to the waistline. Instead of decorating only handbags, small pendants and chain details are attached to belt areas, making the waist a visual focal point.

The trend feels playful, personal, and slightly eccentric. It works best as a moving detail that adds personality without changing the full outfit formula.

## How to style it

Waist Charms work especially well with reduced looks. Simple jeans, a white tee, or a plain skirt gain character immediately. They also pair well with low-waist denim and cargo styles.

## Styling tip

Keep the base look clean. A simple belt and neutral basics let the charms stand out without making the outfit feel overloaded.
