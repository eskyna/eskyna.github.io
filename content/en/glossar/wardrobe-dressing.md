## Definition

**Wardrobe Dressing** is a style approach where versatility and real-life wearability matter more than isolated trend items.

## In simple words

It means buying and styling with your whole wardrobe in mind: fit, repeat colors, combination value, and actual frequency of wear.

## ESKYNA extra: The three-life check

A strong piece can serve at least three contexts, for example day, work, and evening.

**ESKYNA line:** Wardrobe Dressing asks: how often can I be myself in this piece?

## Styling tip

Before buying, name three existing items you can combine with it immediately. If you cannot, wait.
