## Definition

Classic models differentiate between phases such as introduction, growth, widespread acceptance and decline. Digital media can accelerate these processes, multiply them or divide them into many small micro-trends.

## In simple words

Not every trend disappears completely. Some shapes become basics, others return changed after years. What is crucial for your personal wardrobe is whether a trend complements your own style identity or only attracts short-term attention.

## What to pay attention to

- Ask if you liked the item before it became widely visible.
- First test a trend using accessories, colors or second-hand items.
- Rate how well it works with at least three existing outfits.

## Common misconceptions

A trend cycle does not have a fixed duration. Some ideas last weeks, others last decades.

## ESKYNA mnemonic

> Trends move the market - your style decides which movement you follow.
