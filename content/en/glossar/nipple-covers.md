Nipple covers and pasties are thin concealment layers that reduce show-through under fine or semi-transparent fabrics.

## Definition

They are available in silicone, fabric, and disposable formats. Their main purpose is coverage and surface smoothing, not lift or shaping. Visibility depends on edge thickness, finish, and fabric density.

## In Simple Words

Nipple covers conceal. They do not replace support systems.

## Why This Term Matters

With silk, jersey, or flash photography, small surface details strongly affect final polish. Wrong size or edge profile can become visible.

## Consultation Perspective

In consultation, we test material and diameter with the actual top so the cover disappears instead of creating a circular outline.

## Practical Styling Use

- choose a diameter with soft edge transition
- apply to clean, dry skin
- test visibility in daylight and flash
- check comfort for planned wear duration
- clean and store reusable models correctly

- test fit and movement
- check behavior in real light
- verify comfort over several hours
- combine with existing wardrobe pieces

## Concrete Example

Under thin jersey, a larger soft-edge cover often reads smoother than a small thick-edge option.

## Typical Misunderstanding

Not all covers are invisible. Fabric type and edge design decide visibility.

## Distinction from Similar Terms

Boob tape: can add directional support.

Adhesive bra: combines cover and moderate shape control.

Bralette: visible lingerie choice, not invisible concealment.

## ESKYNA Key Takeaway

Good covers disappear visually and keep focus on the outfit.

## Decision Aid Before Purchase or Styling

- is the edge truly invisible under your fabric
- does it stay in place with heat and movement
- is thickness right for your top
- is your skin comfortable with this adhesive
- do you also need support from tape or bra

- does it fit your role and context
- does material behavior match your expectation
- can you combine it at least three ways
