## Definition

A **kaftan**, also spelled **caftan**, is a loose-fitting garment with a straight or flowing silhouette, often cut in a longer length and designed for ease of movement. Depending on its fabric, shape, and finish, it may be worn as a dress, a lightweight outer layer, a lounge piece, or a beach cover-up.

Kaftan forms have varied cultural histories across the Middle East, North Africa, and Central Asia. In contemporary fashion, the word is often used more broadly for flowing garments with wide sleeves, open or softly shaped necklines, and decorative details such as embroidery, trims, or borders.

## Kaftan Explained Simply

Think of a kaftan as an intentionally spacious garment that lets the fabric create the silhouette. Rather than following the body closely, it relies on length, drape, texture, color, and detail to make an impression.

A light cotton or [linen](/en/glossar/leinen/) kaftan can feel relaxed and breathable in warm weather. A version in [silk](/en/glossar/seide/), [satin](/en/glossar/satin/), or a fabric with a refined surface can look much more dressed up. The same basic idea can therefore move from poolside to dinner, depending on styling and context.

The spelling **kaftan** and **caftan** is both common in English. Neither spelling describes one single, fixed global garment shape.

## What Defines a Kaftan?

Most kaftans share several of these features:

- A loose, straight, or softly flowing line
- Generous width through the body
- Long, midi, or maxi lengths
- Wide, dropped, or kimono-like sleeves
- A simple opening, V neckline, round neckline, or open front
- Visible fabric surface, often enhanced by color, print, embroidery, or border details

Some kaftans are entirely unstructured, while others use a tie, seam, or [belt](/en/glossar/guertel/) to create more waist definition. This makes the kaftan adaptable to different styling preferences without losing its characteristic ease.

## How to Style a Kaftan

### As a beach or resort cover-up

For a warm-weather setting, choose a lightweight kaftan over swimwear. A breathable fabric, easy sleeve opening, and practical length matter more than elaborate decoration. Flat sandals, a woven bag, and sunglasses keep the result relaxed and suitable for [resort casual](/en/glossar/resort-casual/) dressing.

### As a summer dress

A more opaque kaftan can be worn on its own as a summer dress. Look for a neckline and shoulder area that feel secure, then consider whether you prefer the garment loose or with a belt at the waist. A defined waist can change the perceived [proportion](/en/glossar/proportion/) of the whole outfit.

### For an elegant occasion

A long kaftan in a refined material can become an evening-focused look, especially when the fabric has depth, a smooth drape, or carefully finished details. Keep accessories deliberate rather than excessive. A clutch, elegant sandal, or a single piece of [statement jewelry](/en/glossar/statement-schmuck/) may be enough.

### For layered everyday outfits

A shorter or open-front kaftan can also work as a light layer over a simple top and trousers. This approach is useful when you want movement and coverage without the weight of a jacket. It can be styled similarly to a long [tunic](/en/glossar/tunika/) or a relaxed overshirt, but usually creates a more fluid effect.

## What to Look for When Buying a Kaftan

The beauty of a kaftan lies in its ease, but ease still needs considered construction.

- **Check the neckline and shoulders:** These areas help the garment stay in place and prevent the width from looking accidental.
- **Consider the fabric:** Lightweight materials are ideal for heat, but check opacity before wearing the garment as a dress.
- **Assess the length:** A floor-skimming hem can look elegant but may be impractical with flat shoes, stairs, or daily movement.
- **Look at the print scale:** Since a kaftan presents a large fabric area, a print or border will strongly influence the overall look.
- **Decide on waist definition:** A belt, drawstring, or softly shaped seam can create structure if you want a more defined silhouette.
- **Examine finishing details:** Clean hems, balanced trims, and carefully placed decoration make a wide garment look intentional.

## Kaftan, Tunic, and Maxi Dress: What Is the Difference?

A kaftan overlaps with several garment categories, but the terms are not identical.

- A [tunic](/en/glossar/tunika/) is often shorter and may be designed to wear over trousers or leggings.
- A [maxi dress](/en/glossar/maxikleid/) is defined mainly by length and may be fitted, shaped, or flowing.
- A kaftan is typically recognized by its generous width, relaxed drape, and robe-like or dress-like construction.

A garment can combine features of all three. The label is less important than how the cut, fabric, and intended use work together.

## Common Misconceptions

- **“A kaftan is only a beach cover-up.”**  
  Kaftans can be beachwear, but they can also be everyday dresses, travel pieces, loungewear, or elegant occasion garments.

- **“Every loose long dress is a kaftan.”**  
  Not necessarily. A loose dress may share a similar silhouette without having the characteristic construction or cultural reference associated with a kaftan.

- **“Loose means shapeless.”**  
  A kaftan can be intentionally voluminous while still looking balanced. Neckline, shoulder fit, length, fabric drape, and waist placement all create structure.

- **“Kaftan is one uniform traditional garment.”**  
  The term includes varied forms and histories. When a garment has a known cultural origin or specific traditional context, it is respectful to name it accurately rather than treating all flowing garments as interchangeable.

## ESKYNA Key Line

> A kaftan creates impact through ease and fabric presence. The right length, material, and detail turn generous volume into a considered look.

## Advisory View

Choose a kaftan according to where and how you will wear it. For everyday use, prioritize opacity, movement, and easy care. For a more polished effect, focus on refined material, balanced proportions, and one clear focal point rather than adding many competing accessories.
