## Definition

A **Schlubby Shirt** is an intentionally relaxed T-shirt with a soft, easy fit that looks effortless instead of overly polished.

## In simple words

The **Schlubby Shirt** is a modern take on casual dressing. It is usually wider, longer, and softer than a classic tee. It is not body-hugging, but it also does not look randomly oversized. The key is an easy look that feels intentional.

Typical features are soft cotton, dropped shoulders, a straight or boxy cut, and a length that reaches the hip or slightly below. It balances very fitted tops and highly constructed silhouettes.

## How to style it

It becomes especially modern when combined with polished pieces. A Schlubby Shirt works with wide trousers, satin skirts, jeans, bermudas, or under a blazer. The contrast creates a look that feels relaxed but still refined.

## Styling tip

Tuck the front lightly into the waistband or combine it with a skirt, wide trousers, or a short jacket. This keeps the look easy but gives it shape.
