## Definition

A **square neckline**, also known as a **carré neckline**, is a neckline with a horizontal upper edge and distinct corners at the sides. Rather than forming a curve or a point, it creates a clear angular opening across the upper chest.

Square necklines appear on dresses, tops, blouses, knitwear, and fitted bodices. Their exact shape can range from softly rounded at the corners to sharply geometric.

## What does a square neckline look like?

The neckline runs relatively straight from one side of the chest to the other, then turns upward toward the straps or shoulders. It leaves the collarbone and upper chest visible while giving the garment a structured, composed appearance.

Compared with a [V Neckline](/en/glossar/v-ausschnitt/), which directs the eye downward, a square neckline places more visual focus across the upper body. Compared with a [Boat Neck](/en/glossar/u-boot-ausschnitt/), it is usually more angular and more open through the center of the chest.

## How a square neckline affects an outfit

A square neckline can make an outfit feel polished, feminine, modern, romantic, or architectural depending on fabric and styling. A crisp cotton dress with a square neckline feels very different from a soft jersey top or a satin evening dress with the same neckline shape.

Its main visual effects include:

- creating a defined frame around the face, neck, and collarbone
- adding horizontal emphasis across the shoulder and upper chest area
- bringing clarity and structure to softer fabrics or flowing silhouettes
- balancing rounded features elsewhere in an outfit, such as puff sleeves or a full skirt
- making a simple top or dress look more intentional

The actual [Neckline Effect](/en/glossar/ausschnittwirkung/) is never determined by shape alone. Neckline width, neckline depth, strap placement, fabric drape, and the garment's overall [Fit](/en/glossar/passform/) all influence the result.

## Square neckline styling ideas

A square neckline is versatile because it can move easily between casual and occasion dressing.

For everyday wear, try a square-neck top with straight-leg jeans, tailored trousers, or an [A-Line Skirt](/en/glossar/a-linien-rock/). A fine necklace or small hoop earrings can complement the open neckline without competing with it.

For a more feminine or dressy look, a square-neck midi dress, fitted bodice, or [Corset Top](/en/glossar/korsett-top/) creates a defined upper-body line. Add a delicate necklace, a structured bag, or earrings according to the occasion.

For cooler weather, square-neck knitwear can offer a clearer alternative to a crew neck. Make sure the fabric has enough stability to hold the neckline smoothly rather than pulling or collapsing at the corners.

## What to check when trying on a square neckline

A well-fitting square neckline should look clean and stay close to the body without cutting in or gaping.

- The upper edge should sit flat against the chest.
- The corners should remain even on both sides.
- Straps should feel secure without pulling the neckline upward.
- When you move your arms, the neckline should not shift excessively.
- The neckline depth should suit the occasion and your comfort level.
- Check whether a bra, bralette, or [Strapless Bra and Bandeau Bra](/en/glossar/traegerloser-bh/) is appropriate for the garment.

A neckline that wrinkles, stands away from the body, or pulls at one corner may point to a sizing or construction issue rather than a problem with the neckline style itself.

## Common misconceptions

**A square neckline is always formal.**  
Not necessarily. The same neckline can feel casual in rib knit or cotton jersey, polished in poplin, and formal in satin, velvet, or structured evening fabrics.

**A square neckline has one fixed effect.**  
Its impact changes with width and depth. A narrow square neckline can feel contained and neat, while a wide, low version can feel more open and expressive.

**It only works on dresses.**  
Square necklines are also common on tops, bodysuits, camisoles, blouses, and knitwear.

## Square neckline versus similar necklines

A square neckline is easy to confuse with other open neckline shapes:

- A [Sweetheart Neckline](/en/glossar/herz-ausschnitt/) curves over the bust and creates a heart-like outline.
- A [V Neckline](/en/glossar/v-ausschnitt/) narrows toward a central point.
- A [Boat Neck](/en/glossar/u-boot-ausschnitt/) runs wide and horizontally but follows a softer curved line.
- A [Scoop Neckline](/en/glossar/rundhalsausschnitt/) is rounded rather than angular.

The defining feature of the square neckline is its horizontal line paired with visible corners.

## ESKYNA Key Line

A square neckline gives the upper body a clear, geometric frame. The best version is one that sits smoothly, supports your comfort, and matches the mood of the full outfit.
