## Definition

A **costume**, in German-speaking fashion terminology, is a coordinated two-piece ensemble consisting of a jacket and skirt. In English, it is generally described as a **skirt suit** or jacket-skirt suit.

The jacket and skirt are traditionally made in the same fabric, color, and finish, or are clearly designed as a set. A costume is the skirt-based counterpart to a [pants suit](/en/glossar/hosenanzug/). Depending on its cut, material, color, and styling, it can look professional, classic, feminine, festive, modern, or deliberately creative.

## Costume Meaning in Fashion

The word _costume_ can be confusing in English because it often refers to fancy dress, theatrical clothing, or historical dress. In German fashion language, however, it means a tailored or coordinated jacket-and-skirt ensemble.

A costume creates a composed look because its two main pieces already share a visual language. The matching fabric brings calm and structure, while details such as the shoulder line, waist shaping, skirt length, and shoes determine how formal or relaxed the final outfit feels.

A fitted [blazer](/en/glossar/blazer/) with a straight skirt can appear precise and businesslike. A softly cut jacket with a midi skirt may feel more relaxed. Textured fabrics such as [tweed](/en/glossar/tweed/) give the ensemble a classic, tactile character, while clean fabrics and simpler construction can make it feel more contemporary.

## How a Jacket-Skirt Ensemble Changes Your Look

A coordinated jacket and skirt set creates immediate visual unity. This can be useful when you want to look polished without building an outfit from unrelated pieces.

The effect depends particularly on these elements:

- **Jacket length:** A cropped jacket emphasizes the waist and can create a compact silhouette. A longer jacket gives more vertical line and a calmer appearance.
- **Skirt shape:** A [pencil skirt](/en/glossar/bleistiftrock/) looks sleek and tailored, while an [A-Line Skirt](/en/glossar/a-linien-rock/) offers more movement and a softer outline.
- **Waist position:** The relationship between jacket hem and skirt waistband strongly affects [proportion](/en/glossar/proportion/).
- **Fabric and finish:** Smooth, matte fabrics tend to read as more restrained, while bouclé, visible texture, shine, or strong pattern make the set more expressive.
- **Shoes and accessories:** Pumps, a blouse, and a structured bag can make the ensemble suitable for formal work or events. Sneakers, loafers, or a simple T-shirt make it more approachable for everyday wear.

## How to Style a Costume for Different Occasions

### For business and formal settings

For a business setting, choose a well-fitting jacket, a skirt length that feels secure when sitting and walking, and understated accessories. A blouse, fine-knit top, or polished shirt works well underneath. The appropriate level of formality depends on the workplace, so distinguish between [Business Casual](/en/glossar/business-casual/), [Business Professional](/en/glossar/business-professional/), and [Business Formal](/en/glossar/business-formal/).

### For everyday wear

To make a skirt suit feel less formal, separate the set visually with casual elements. Try a plain T-shirt, fine knit, relaxed shoe, or crossbody bag. The jacket can work with jeans, while the skirt can be paired with a sweater or simple top. This makes the costume a more versatile wardrobe investment.

### For celebrations and events

For festive occasions, fabric, color, and accessories can take the lead. A refined blouse, silk-like top, statement earrings, or dressier shoes can elevate a coordinated ensemble. Always consider the invitation and the relevant [dress code](/en/glossar/dresscode/) before styling it as eveningwear.

## What to Look for When Buying a Skirt Suit

A matching set should look coherent, but it also needs to function in real life. Before buying, check the following:

- Try the jacket closed and open. The shoulder line, bust area, sleeves, and button placement should feel comfortable.
- Sit down in the skirt. Check the length, movement allowance, waistband comfort, and whether the skirt rides up.
- Look at the full outfit in motion, not only while standing still.
- Assess whether the jacket hem and skirt waistband create a balanced line.
- Test the jacket and skirt separately with pieces you already own. A coordinated ensemble becomes more useful when both elements are strong [combination pieces](/en/glossar/kombinationsstueck/).
- Consider fabric care and durability, especially if the set is intended for frequent workwear use.

## Common Misunderstandings

- **A costume is always conservative or old-fashioned.**  
  Not necessarily. Modern proportions, minimalist fabrics, unexpected footwear, and relaxed styling can make a jacket-skirt ensemble feel current.

- **The jacket and skirt must always be worn together.**  
  No. Wearing the pieces separately often improves their [versatility](/en/glossar/kombinierbarkeit/) and increases the value of the purchase.

- **A costume is the same as a pants suit.**  
  Both are coordinated tailored ensembles, but a costume includes a skirt. A pants suit includes trousers and creates a different silhouette and practical experience.

- **Matching fabric automatically guarantees a good fit.**  
  A set can coordinate perfectly and still feel uncomfortable. [Fit](/en/glossar/passform/), ease of movement, and proportion matter as much as visual harmony.

## ESKYNA Key Line

> A costume brings structure through coordination, but it becomes truly versatile when jacket and skirt also work confidently on their own.

## Advisory View

A jacket-skirt ensemble can be a reliable part of a considered [wardrobe](/en/glossar/garderobe/), especially when it suits your real occasions and personal style. Choose it for the way you want to feel and move, not simply because it appears formally correct.
