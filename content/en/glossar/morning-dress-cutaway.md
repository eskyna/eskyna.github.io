## Definition

Classic items for men include a black or gray morning coat, a vest, striped or subtly patterned trousers, a shirt, a tie and formal shoes. Depending on the invitation, women are expected to wear formal daytime clothing, often with a hat or fascinator.

## In simple words

The name does not mean that the dress code is only worn early in the morning. This refers to formal clothing for daytime events, such as certain weddings, ceremonial occasions or traditional receptions. In the evening you usually change to an evening dress code.

## What to pay attention to

- Read the invitation carefully and focus on the location, host and tradition of the event.
- Pay attention to the correct trouser length, calm fabrics and an overall well-groomed appearance.
- Do not confuse the cutaway with a tailcoat or regular frock coat.

## Common misconceptions

Morning dress is not a business suit with a vest and is not limited to before noon.

## ESKYNA mnemonic
