## Definition

It runs parallel to the chain and usually frays less than a cut edge. Depending on the material, it can be narrow, colored, printed or particularly tightly woven.

## In simple words

The selvedge helps to identify the straight direction of the thread. In selvedge denim, it is deliberately used visibly, for example on a folded trouser hem. However, it is not automatically suitable for normal seams because it can be tighter and distort the fabric.

## What to pay attention to

- Use the selvedge as a guide for the direction of the thread, not blindly as a finished seam edge.
- Check if it pulls the fabric together or is a different color.
- If selvedge processing is visible, ensure clean, symmetrical placement.

## Common misconceptions

Selvage and hem are not the same thing. The selvedge is created during fabric production, the hem is created on the garment.

## ESKYNA mnemonic

> The selvedge shows how the fabric is constructed - it is only used visibly through a conscious design decision.
