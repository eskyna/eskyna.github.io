## Definition

A **Balloon Hem** is a gathered or inward-folded hem that creates a rounded, voluminous shape on skirts, dresses, or tops.

## In simple words

The **Balloon Hem** is a clear construction detail that adds immediate volume and a sculptural silhouette. Instead of falling flat, the hem curves outward or is drawn inward for a balloon effect.

You often see it in mini skirts, dresses, and statement summer pieces. It looks playful and feminine while still feeling modern through its clean shape.

## How to style it

Let the hem stay the focal point. Since it already brings volume, simple tops, reduced shoes, and clear accessories keep the outfit balanced.

## Styling tip

Pair it with calm counterparts: a plain top, a short jacket, flat sandals, or minimalist sneakers.
