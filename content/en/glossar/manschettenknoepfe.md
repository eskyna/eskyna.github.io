## Definition

They are primarily worn with fold-over or double cuffs, but can also fit special combination cuffs. Shapes range from simple metal to gemstones, enamel or personal motifs.

## In simple words

Because cufflinks are visible close to the hands and watch, they add a formal level of detail. Small, quiet models appear close to business; eye-catching motifs of a more personal or festive nature. The mechanism should close securely and not get caught on fabric or skin.

## What to pay attention to

- Check whether the shirt has real cufflink openings.
- Match metal tones to the watch, belt buckle and other jewelry details.
- Pay attention to size and weight so that the cuff does not twist.

## Common misconceptions

Cufflinks do not replace a normal button on every cuff. The cuff shape must be designed for this.

## ESKYNA note

> Cufflinks are small details that are very close to the gesture - when chosen precisely, they appear personal rather than decorative.
