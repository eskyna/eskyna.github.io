## Definition

They often run across or diagonally and point to the area where material is being held or displaced. Location and direction help diagnose fit problems.

## In simple words

Horizontal folds can indicate narrow width, diagonal lines can indicate a problem with the shape of the chest, hips, shoulders or crotch. Not every wrinkle is a mistake: movement, soft fabrics and conscious draping also create lines. What matters is whether they are persistent, tense and disruptive.

## What to pay attention to

- Observe the fold in a relaxed position and after movement.
- Follow their direction to the point where fabric is visibly pulling.
- Check the size and cut first before tightening or widening individual seams.

## Common misconceptions

Pull creases are not just normal creases. They show recurring tension or distribution in the section.

## ESKYNA mnemonic

> Drawstring folds are visible clues: They show where a piece of clothing needs more space or a different shape.
