## Definition

Typical features include low-waist silhouettes, short tops, cargo details, shiny materials, small shoulder bags, tinted glasses and a playful digital aesthetic.

## In simple words

The style thrives on nostalgia, pop culture and a mix of futuristic and deliberately kitschy details. For a wearable look, a single signal is often enough. Anyone who adopts all the codes at the same time quickly appears disguised or loses sight of their own proportions.

## What to pay attention to

- Choose one or two Y2K items that fit your wardrobe today.
- Check waist height and top length in motion, not just in photos.
- Combine nostalgic accessories with current, high-quality basics.

## Common misconceptions

Y2K is not every fashion from the 2000s. The term describes a specific world of images that is strongly influenced by pop culture and digital.

## ESKYNA mnemonic

> Y2K seems modern when the memory is quoted and not completely replayed.
