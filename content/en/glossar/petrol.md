## Definition

**Petrol Blue**: Petrol Blue is a color term that helps you plan outfits with clearer harmony, contrast, and mood.

## In simple words

Petrol Blue gives you a practical language for seeing clothing more clearly. It is not about following a rigid rule. It is about understanding what a garment, color, detail, or styling choice does inside the full look.

At ESKYNA, this term is always connected with personality, everyday life, and desired impact. Style should not make you feel restricted; it should make decisions easier and your presence more precise.

## Why this matters in color styling

When you understand Petrol Blue, you can choose clothing more deliberately. You see whether a piece only looks interesting on its own or whether it truly supports your wardrobe, your occasion, and the impression you want to create.

With Petrol Blue, pay attention to temperature, depth, saturation, and the colors worn close to the face. A shade can look elegant in one combination and flat in another.

## What to pay attention to

- Check the role of Petrol Blue in the full outfit.
- Look at color, material, cut, proportion, and occasion together.
- Ask whether the choice supports your desired impact.
- Prefer clarity over random details.
- Use the term as a decision aid when shopping or combining outfits.

## Common misconceptions

**Misconception:** Petrol Blue is only a fashion word.  
**Better:** It describes a real styling cue that can influence fit, mood, quality, combination, or personal presence.

**Misconception:** Petrol Blue works the same way for everyone.  
**Better:** Its effect depends on your proportions, color direction, lifestyle, context, and the other pieces in the outfit.

## Quick mirror check

Look at “Petrol Blue” once up close and once from a little distance. Up close you see detail, color, and material; from a distance you see whether the overall impression stays clear, calm, and coherent. If the look only works from one perspective, it probably needs more balance.

## ESKYNA note

Petrol Blue works best when it feels intentional rather than accidental. The strongest looks usually come from clarity, proportion, and a calm connection between the person and the clothes.
