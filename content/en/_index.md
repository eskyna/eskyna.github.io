faq:
  title: Frequently asked questions before you start
  intro: Here you will find answers to common questions.
  items:
    - q: Is the coaching suitable for me even if I have not focused much on style before?
      a:
        Yes. It is especially helpful if you want more clarity in everyday life, fewer bad purchases, and a more
        coherent presence without needing to dive deeply into fashion.
    - q: What happens in the free initial consultation?
      a:
        In the free initial consultation, we discuss your current situation, your wishes, and the most sensible next
        step. That way, we can figure out together which offer fits you best.
    - q: How do I know which offer is right for me?
      a:
        That is exactly what the free initial consultation is for. We clarify whether a style test, style analysis,
        wardrobe check, or shopping support makes sense for your situation.
    - q: Do I need to replace my entire wardrobe?
      a:
        No. In many cases, we can achieve much more with what you already own. New purchases are only recommended
        where they meaningfully support your style.
    - q: What is better for me, online or in person?
      a:
        Online is ideal if you want flexible advice regardless of location. In person is especially helpful for wardrobe
        work, specific outfit combinations, or personal shopping support.
    - q: Is the coaching useful in a professional context too?
      a:
        Yes. If your appearance at work matters, the coaching helps make competence, personality, and presence more
        visible. This is especially valuable for meetings, client appointments, presentations, and leadership roles.
